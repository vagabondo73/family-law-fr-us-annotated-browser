511.020 — Judgment defined.

in forceIn force since : 1939-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=511.020 · Interactive browser

Current text

A "judgment" is the final determination of the right of the parties in the action.
