74.11 — Satisfaction of Judgment

in forceIn force since : 2019-07-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199751 · Rule 74 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

(a) Acknowledgment of Satisfaction. When any judgment or decree is satisfied, the judgment creditor shall immediately file an acknowledgment of satisfaction.

(b) Who May Enter Satisfaction. Satisfaction may be entered by the judgment creditor, his attorney of record, or an agent; if entered by an agent who is not the attorney of record, his authority shall be filed. (c) Satisfaction of Judgment Entered by the Court. If a judgment creditor who has received satisfaction of a judgment fails to acknowledge such satisfaction immediately, any interested person may apply to the court where the judgment was entered for an order showing satisfaction.
