512.270 — Judge assigned to hear case anew.

in forceIn force since : 1979-01-02 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=512.270 · Interactive browser

Current text

The judge assigned to hear the cause shall proceed to hear, try and determine the same anew with a record of the proceedings being made, without regarding any error, defect or other imperfection on the trial, judgment or other proceedings of the associate circuit judge in relation to the cause.

Correspondences (CPC ↔ Missouri)

CPCMissouriEquivalenceFRCPNote
art. 638 · Légifrance512.270, 512.160partial—The case is reheard on fact and law by the remand court, except for issues unaffected by the reversal. Missouri provides for a case to be heard anew and for the remand court to implement the appellate disposition, but does not expressly state the limitation to reversed issues.
