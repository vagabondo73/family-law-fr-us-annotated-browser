508.011 — Change of venue, state statute prevails if in conflict with supreme court rules.

in forceIn force since : 2005-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=508.011 · Interactive browser

Current text

To the extent that rule 51.03 of the Missouri rules of civil procedure contradicts any provision of this chapter, the provisions of this chapter shall prevail regarding any tort claim.

Summary (non-official) : Supreme Court of Missouri opinion applying or construing Rule 51.03, § 508.011 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

To hold that chapter 508 and Rule 51.03 contradict each other would require the Court to determine the authority of the legislature to change a procedural rule by the method used in this instance.
The relators incorrectly contend that section 508.011 prevents such a transfer because section 508.010.4 requires that venue for such a suit is where the plaintiff was first injured by the wrongful acts or negligent conduct alleged in the action.
The relators contend that section 508.011 prohibits application of Rule 51.03.
