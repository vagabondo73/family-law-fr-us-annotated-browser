67.04 — Dismissal of Counterclaim, Cross-Claim or Third-Party Clam – Rules Applicable

in forceIn force since : 1973-09-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199710 · Rule 67 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

The provisions of Rules 67.01, 67.02, 67.03 and 67.05 apply to the dismissal of any counterclaim, cross-claim or third-party claim.

See also : 67.01 (mo-rules), 67.02 (mo-rules), 67.03 (mo-rules), 67.05 (mo-rules)

Ellison v. Fry, 565 S.W.3d 699 (Mo. App. S.D. 2018)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Southern District · 2018-11-02 · No. SD 35394 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Southern District opinion applying or construing Rule 74.04, Rule 67.02, Rule 67.04 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

On April 21, 2016, after a hearing on Figg's motion, the trial court entered its order notifying the parties that because the parties had briefed and argued matters outside the pleadings, it was the trial court's intent to treat Figg's motion as a partial summary judgment motion, pursuant to Rule 74.04.
On February 14, 2018, Counterclaim Plaintiffs filed a "Notice of Voluntary Dismissal Without Prejudice as to all Counterclaim Defendants," wherein Counterclaim Plaintiffs stated that they were "dismiss[ing] their Petition against Beverly Figg, and Counter-Petition as to all Counterclaim Defendants, without prejudice[.]" See Rule 67.02(a) (authorizing a claimant to dismiss its claim "without order of the court [at] anytime[,]" subject to exceptions inapplicable here); Rule 67.04 (indicating, in relevant part, that the provisions of Rule 67.02 "apply to the dismissal of any counterclaim.").

Palisades Collection, LLC v. Watson, 375 S.W.3d 857 (Mo. App. W.D. 2012)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 2012-08-14 · No. WD 74533 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing § 512.020 RSMo, Rule 67.01, Rule 67.03, Rule 67.04 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

“We acquire jurisdiction as soon as the trial court issues a ‘final judgment.’ ” Id. (citing section 512.020(5)).
That is because “[a] dismissal without prejudice permits the party to bring another civil action for the same cause, unless the civil action is otherwise barred.” Rule 67.01.
Rule 67.04 provides that the provisions of Rule 67.03 apply equally to the dismissal of counterclaims.
