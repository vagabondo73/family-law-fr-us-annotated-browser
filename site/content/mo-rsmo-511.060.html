511.060 — When set aside.

in forceIn force since : 1957-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=511.060 · Interactive browser

Current text

The judgment may, for good cause shown, be set aside at any time within thirty days after it is entered, upon such terms as are just.

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing § 511.060 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Section 511.060 RSMo 1986 and Rule 75.01 allow the trial court thirty days to set aside a judgment which finally disposed of the case.
Pursuant to § 511.060 RSMo 1986 and Rule 75.01, a trial court retains control thirty days after the date of judgment.
