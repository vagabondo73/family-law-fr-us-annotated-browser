451.010 — Marriage a civil contract.

in forceIn force since : 1939-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=451.010 · Interactive browser

Current text

Marriage is considered in law as a civil contract, to which the consent of the parties capable in law of contracting is essential.
