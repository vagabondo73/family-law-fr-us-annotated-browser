509.040 — Technical forms not required in pleading.

in forceIn force since : 1943-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=509.040 · Interactive browser

Current text

Each averment of a pleading shall be simple, concise, and direct. No technical forms of pleading or motions are required.

Korn v. Ray, 434 S.W.2d 798 (Mo. App. 1968)

St. Louis Court of Appeals · 1968-11-19 · No. 32993 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : St. Louis Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing § 511.250 RSMo, § 509.110 RSMo, § 509.040 RSMo, § 506.100 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

In ruling in the affirmative the court held that a judgment entered in the equitable proceeding had properly been set aside since it was a personal judgment in favor of all of the separate lien claimants jointly against all defendants, including trustees and mortgagees, without ordering a lien on any property and the judgment was coram non judice and void; and was subject to being set aside on motion of any interested party under § 511.250, RS Mo 1959, V.A.M.S. (Quaere) Ruckman v. Ruckman, Mo.App., 337 S.W.2d 100, ruled there was no irregularity justifying motion under § 511.250, supra, where in response to timely motion for new trial the court, more than 30 days after original judgment, set aside the original judgment and entered a new judgment.
Here plaintiff had a single cause of action which she pleaded against Model and its co-defendant in Counts II and III. This court ruled in American Displays v. E. T. Swiney Motors, Mo.App., 240 S.W.2d 732, that when a plaintiff has but a single cause of action which may be stated in different ways so as to meet different phases of the evidence as the same may possibly develop at the trial, the plaintiff may state such cause of action in different counts, and cannot be compelled to elect upon which count he will proceed. This contention of Model likewise ignores Civil Rule 55.12, V.A.M.R., and § 509.110, RSMo 1959, V.A.M.S., which authorize and permit alternative pleading of a claim.
Civil Rule 55.05, V.A.M.R.; § 509.040, RSMo 1959, V.A.M.S. The prayer for relief is not a part of the pleading and the omission thereof does not oust the court of jurisdiction on the subject matter.

Walker v. Kansas City Star Co., 406 S.W.2d 44 (Mo. 1966)

Supreme Court of Missouri · 1966-07-11 · No. 51705 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Supreme Court of Missouri opinion applying or construing § 509.040 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Certainly, plaintiff’s pleading is “simple, concise and direct” as required by Section 509.040, V.A.M.S., in leveling a charge of wanton and willful malice at defendants, and until plaintiff is permitted a trial on the merits where he can offer whatever evidence he may have to support his charge it cannot be said that he has failed to make a case of actual malice.

As provided in § 510.310, all fact issues are therefore “deemed found in accordance with the result reached.” See also Bank of Brimson v. Graham, 335 Mo. 1196, 76 S.W.2d 376, 382, 96 A.L.R. 399.
In this day of simplified pleadings, where a statement of the facts is the ultimate requirement, and technical forms are not required (§ 509.050, § 509.-250, § 509.040), it would be hypertech-nical to adjudge this pleading insufficient as a plea of either waiver or estoppel.

Dugan v. Trout, 271 S.W.2d 593 (Mo. App. 1954)

Springfield Court of Appeals · 1954-09-23 · Nos. 7233, 7234 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Springfield Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing § 510.290 RSMo, § 509.090 RSMo, § 509.250 RSMo, § 509.040 RSMo, § 509.400 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

The jury returned verdicts finding for plaintiff in the sum of $1,000 on Count I but finding “that the defendant is entitled to the possession of the hay baler” on Count II. The court thereafter overruled plaintiff’s timely after-trial motion (under Section 510.290) for judgment in accordance with his motions for directed verdicts filed at the close of all of the evidence, but plaintiff’s alternative motion for new trial was sustained as to both counts “because the two verdicts are inconsistent and contradictory.”
Furthermore, since Section 509.400 requires that “(a) 11 defenses and objections for which there is no provision for the raising of the same by motion shall be raised in the responsive pleading” and since Section 509.090 provides that waiver or “any other matter constituting an avoidance or affirmative defense” shall be set forth affirmatively "in pleading to a preceding pleading,” any affirmative matter by which defendant seeks to defeat plaintiff’s recovery should be pleaded in the answer, and not in a pleading wholly unresponsive to the petition.
Section 509.250; DeVault v. Truman, 354 Mo. 1193, 194 S.W.2d 29, 32.
