marital-presumption-burden — Presumption of marital property — clear and convincing evidence to rebut

in forceIn force since : 2002-06-03 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courtlistener.com/c/S.W.3d/108/1/ · Leading source: James v. James, 108 S.W.3d 1 (Mo. banc 2002) · Interactive browser

Summary rule

“[a] party who claims that property, presumed to be marital, is in fact separate property assumes the burden of rebutting the presumption with clear and convincing evidence” (James v. James, Mo. banc 2002, applying § 452.330.3).
