55.17 — Official Documents or Acts – Form of Pleading

in forceIn force since : 1973-09-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199648 · Rule 55 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

In pleading an official document or official act it is sufficient to aver that the document was issued or that the act was done in compliance with law.

Issues : mo.procedure.pleadings-motions, proc.mo.pleadings-motions.pleading-form
