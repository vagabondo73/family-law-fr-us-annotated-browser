511.190 — Judgment absolute after three years.

in forceIn force since : 1939-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=511.190 · Interactive browser

Current text

If such petition for review be not filed within three years after such final judgment is rendered, the same shall stand absolute, whether notice thereof be given or not.

State ex rel. Rhine v. Montgomery, 422 S.W.2d 661 (Mo. App. 1967)

Springfield Court of Appeals · 1967-12-12 · No. 8648 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Springfield Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing § 511.190 RSMo, § 511.170 RSMo, § 511.200 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

“This statute [§ 511.190 and Rule 74.14] applies only to the special procedure of petition for review of a valid default judgment, provided in [§ 511.170 and Rule 74.12] based upon the grounds set out in [§ 511.200 and Rule 74.15] * * *. It is not applicable to a judgment void for lack of jurisdiction to enter it, because such a judgment is of no force and effect whatever and may be collaterally attacked.” Hankins v. Smarr, 345 Mo. 973, 137 S.W.2d 499, 501(3).
Respondent also contended- in the circuit court the magistrate judgment was not subject to attack as it became absolute aftei three years under § 511.190 and Rule 74.14.
