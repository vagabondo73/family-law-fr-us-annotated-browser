506.170 — Where process may be served.

in forceIn force since : 1943-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=506.170 · Interactive browser

Current text

All process may be served anywhere within the territorial limits of the state and may be forwarded to the sheriff of any county for the purpose of service.
