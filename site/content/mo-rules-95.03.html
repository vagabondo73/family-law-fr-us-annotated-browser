95.03 — Petition for Change of a Minor's Name – Notice to or Consent of Parents

in forceIn force since : 1981-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199935 · Interactive browser

Current text

When a minor files a petition for change of name the written consent of each known parent shall be filed. If the written consent of each known parent is not filed, a copy of the petition together with a notice stating the date of the hearing shall be served on each known parent not consenting not less than thirty days before the date of the hearing. The service shall be by registered or certified mail addressed to the last known address of the nonconsenting parent. Service shall be proved by the certificate of the clerk that he has mailed a copy of the petition and notice by registered or certified mail.

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is new.

Rule 95.03 reads, in part: "When a minor files a petition for change of name the written consent of each known parent shall be filed.
If the written consent of each known parent is not filed, a copy of the petition together with a notice stating the date of the hearing shall be served on each known parent not consent-ing_" Rule 95.04 reads: “The court shall order the desired change of name if it finds that the change would be proper and not detrimental to the interests of any other person.”
A trial court has discretion under Rules 95.03 and 95.04 to deny a petition if there is evidence that the change would be detrimental to the interests of other persons.
