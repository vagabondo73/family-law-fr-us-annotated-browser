51.14 — Jurisdiction of Judge and Court in Civil Actions Removed

in forceIn force since : 1994-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199590 · Rule 51 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

(a) Following a change of venue, for any reason, to a county outside the circuit from which venue was changed, the case shall be handled by a judge regularly appointed to hear cases in the new circuit, unless a judge is otherwise assigned to hear the case as authorized by article V, §§ 6 or 15 of the constitution. (b) The court to which any civil actions shall be transferred by change of venue shall have jurisdiction to hear and determine the same, and shall proceed to final judgment and execution therein, although such civil action would not otherwise be cognizable in such court.

STATE OF MISSOURI, ex rel. RUDY TAPIA and DOUGLAS ROGERS, Relators v. THE HONORABLE DEREK ANKROM, Judge of the Thirty-First Judicial Circuit, Greene County, Missouri, No. SD38681 (Mo. App. S.D. 2025)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Southern District · 2025-04-11 · SD38681 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Southern District opinion applying or construing § 508.010 RSMo, Rule 51.04, Rule 51.05, Rule 51.02, Rule 51.06, Rule 51.07 and others (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

In relevant part, § 508.010 states: Notwithstanding any other provision of law, in all actions in which there is any count alleging a tort and in which the plaintiff was first injured in the state of Missouri, venue shall be in the county where the plaintiff was first injured by the acts or conduct alleged in the action.
In relevant part, Rule 51.045(a) requires any motion to transfer venue alleging improper venue to be filed within 60 days of service on the party seeking transfer, unless the court extends the time for good cause shown.
In relevant part, Rule 51.05(a) states that “[a] change of judge shall be ordered in any civil action upon the timely filing of a written application therefor by a party. … The application need not allege or prove any cause for such change of judge and need not be verified.” Id. Pursuant to Rule 51.05(b), the application “must be filed within 60 days from service of process or 30 days from the designation of the trial judge, whichever is longer.”

State ex rel. Lebanon School District R-III v. Winfrey, 183 S.W.3d 232 (Mo. 2006)

Supreme Court of Missouri · 2006-01-31 · No. SC 86873 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Supreme Court of Missouri opinion applying or construing Rule 51.03, Rule 51.01, Rule 51.04, § 508.140 RSMo, § 508.250 RSMo, Rule 51.14 and others (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Rule 51.03 does not state that it does not apply where one or more defendants is a municipal corporation that could originally be sued only in the county in which suit was commenced. To the contrary, Rule 51.03(c) states, “If a timely application is filed, the court immediately shall order the case transferred to some other county convenient to the parties, first giving all parties the opportunity to make suggestions as to where the case should be sent.”
Lebanon argues that this Court should nonetheless read an implicit exception into Rule 51.03 if a school district or other municipal corporation is a defendant because to do otherwise would contravene Rule 51.01, which states, “These rules should not be construed to extend or limit the jurisdiction of the courts of Missouri, or the venue of civil actions therein.”
If a party believes that suit has been filed against it in a venue not permitted by statute, then Rule 51.045 provides the procedural mechanism by which the case may be transferred to a county in which suit would have been proper.

State ex rel. Montgomery v. Knust, 740 S.W.2d 405 (Mo. App. 1987)

Missouri Court of Appeals · 1987-11-25 · No. 15279 published
Basis (b): identical text — text-identical

Justification : The opinion (1987-11-25) predates the current version of Rule 51.14 (in force since 1994-01-01) but quotes the language it applies — “The court to which any civil actions shall be transferred by change of venue shall have jurisdiction to hear and determine the same, and shall proceed to final judgment and execution therein, although such civil action would not otherwise be cognizable in such court.” — and that language appears verbatim (normalized for punctuation/case) in the current text; the interpreted language is therefore identical in wording and function.

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing Rule 51.02, Rule 51.14, § 508.080 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

On January 30, 1987, relator filed in the Circuit Court of Cole County an instrument denominated “Application For Change of Venue” stating, in pertinent part: “Comes Now, C.H. Montgomery ... and pursuant to Supreme Court Rule 51.02, Change Of Venue by Agreement, petitions this court to issue its order and to have this cause of action ... Transferred to the County of Webster, Marshfield, Missouri ... wherein the jurisdiction and venue now lies.
Relator’s petition prayed that we prohibit respondent “from dismissing Relator’s Petition for Dissolution of Marriage which was ‘transfered’ [sic] to the Circuit Court of Webster County from the Circuit Court of Cole County ... which was ... filed ... as CV987-108-DR, pursuant to Rule 51.14 of the Missouri Rules of Civil Procedures.”
That being so, the purported transfer cannot be sustained under § 508.080.
