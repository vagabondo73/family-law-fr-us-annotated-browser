96.06 — Who Shall be Made Parties

in forceIn force since : 1981-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199944 · Rule 96 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

Every person having an interest in the land, whether in possession or otherwise, shall be made a party to an action in partition.

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is substantially the same as prior Rule 96.06.

Issues : proc.mo.special-actions.ejectment-land-titles

Hilton v. Crouch, 627 S.W.2d 99 (Mo. App. 1982)

Missouri Court of Appeals · 1982-01-05 · No. 12337 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing Rule 96.29, Rule 96.06, Rule 96.05, Rule 96.08 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Rule 96.29 deals with that situation and provides safeguards for the protection of creditors.
Rule 96.05 requires the petition to contain a legal description of “the land” sought to be partitioned. Rule 96.06 requires the joinder of every person having an interest in “the land.”
Rule 96.08 requires the court to determine the interests of the parties and order partition in kind or the sale of “the land.”
