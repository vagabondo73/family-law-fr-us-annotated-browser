515.040 — Referees to be disinterested parties.

in forceIn force since : 1939-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=515.040 · Interactive browser

Current text

No person shall be appointed referee who is of kin to either party, or is interested in the event of the action.
