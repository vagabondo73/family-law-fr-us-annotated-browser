51.07 — When Judge Disqualified Without Application

in forceIn force since : 1994-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199583 · Rule 51 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

If the judge is interested or related to any party or shall have been counsel in the civil action, or is recused for any reason, the judge promptly shall transfer the case to the presiding judge of the circuit for reassignment in accordance with the procedures of Rule 51.05(e).

Correspondences (CPC ↔ Missouri)

CPCMissouriEquivalenceFRCPNote
art. 339 · Légifrance51.07, 51.05equivalent—A judge who believes recusal is warranted is replaced by another judge designated by the court’s presiding authority. Rule 51.07 requires a recused judge to transfer the case to the circuit’s presiding judge for reassignment under Rule 51.05(e).
art. 341 · Légifrance508.090, 51.07, 508.100partial—Article 341 refers the grounds for recusal to article L. 111-6 of the French Code of Judicial Organization. The Missouri provisions list certain disqualification grounds and provide for reassignment or appointment of another judge, but the grounds and mechanisms are not identical.
art. 347 · Légifrance51.07, 51.05partial—The article provides for replacement of a recused judge or transfer for legitimate suspicion, together with effects on prior acts and decisions. Rules 51.07 and 51.05 provide reassignment or change of judge, but do not reproduce all of those effects.
art. 350 · Légifrance51.07, 508.100functional—Article 350 creates a special procedure before the first president of the Court of Cassation for challenging an appellate chief judge or seeking transfer of an entire appellate court. The Missouri provisions instead provide reassignment or transfer when a judge is disqualified, without the same institutional procedure.
art. 1027 · Légifrance51.07partial—Article 1027 requires a recusal request concerning a Court of Cassation judge to be brought before the first president, by a specialized counsel where representation is mandatory. Rule 51.07 addresses judicial recusal by requiring transfer to the circuit’s presiding judge for reassignment, but uses a different mechanism and concerns a different court level.
