455.007 — Mootness doctrine, public interest exception to apply upon expiration of the order.

in forceIn force since : 2014-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=455.007 · Revisor — this version (bid) · Interactive browser

Current text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law to the contrary, the public interest exception to the mootness doctrine shall apply to an appeal of a full order of protection which has expired.

The Western District recently addressed the issue of mootness in relation to an expired order of protection and held “[a]s there is no other provision of law to the contrary, we ‘shall’ apply the public interest exception to the mootness doctrine [in Section 455.007] to the trial court’s judgment granting a full order of protection.”
We recognize that in an appeal arguing sufficiency of the evidence, B.A.G. did not have incentive to include all of the evidence admitted at trial, nor was it explicitly required by Rule 81.12.
Enacted in 2011, Section 455.007 stated the following: Notwithstanding any other provision of law to the contrary, the public interest exception to the mootness doctrine shall apply to an appeal of a full order of protection which; (1) [h]as expired; and (2) [s]ubjects, the person against whom such order is issued to significant collateral consequences by the mere existence of such full order of protection after its expiration.
The Missouri General Assembly amended Section 455.007 in 2014, deleting the language in subsection (2).
