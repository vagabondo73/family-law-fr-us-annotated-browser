511.060 — When set aside.

in forceIn force since : 1957-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=511.060 · Interactive browser

Current text

The judgment may, for good cause shown, be set aside at any time within thirty days after it is entered, upon such terms as are just.

In the matter of: Lauren Micaela Taylor...., No. ED113222 (Mo. App. E.D. 2025)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District · 2025-11-18 · ED113222 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District opinion applying or construing Rule 74.14, § 511.760 RSMo, § 511.060 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Appellants' contend (1) the Probate Court had authority to vacate the Georgia Court orders because the case was transferred to it under the Uniform Adult Guardianship and Protective Proceedings Act (UAGPPJA) (§§ 475.531, 475.532) and the Uniform Enforcement of Foreign Judgments Law (Rule 74.14; 6 § 511.760); (2) any prior termination of Lauren's guardianship and conservatorship did not bar a subsequent motion to set aside; and (3) alternatively, the Probate Court did not properly terminate Lauren's guardianship and conservatorship because it failed to follow the transfer requirements of the UAGPPJA. Here, a review of the record reveals the Probate Court's October 29, 2024 order does not constitute a final appealable judgment pursuant to Missouri law.
No, a judicial confirmation of a probate commissioner's order must occur within 30 days of its issuance to be considered a final appealable judgment under §§ 478.266.1 and 511.060.

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing § 511.060 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Section 511.060 RSMo 1986 and Rule 75.01 allow the trial court thirty days to set aside a judgment which finally disposed of the case.
Pursuant to § 511.060 RSMo 1986 and Rule 75.01, a trial court retains control thirty days after the date of judgment.
