509.130 — Adoption of statements by reference — exhibits.

in forceIn force since : 1943-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=509.130 · Interactive browser

Current text

Statements in a pleading may be adopted by reference in a different part of the same pleading or in another pleading or in any motion. An exhibit to a pleading is a part thereof for all purposes.
