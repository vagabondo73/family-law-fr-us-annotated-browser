94.07 — Answer – Motions

in forceIn force since : 1981-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199931 · Rule 94 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

The answer shall be directed to the petition in mandamus. The answer may include or be accompanied by one or more motions.

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is new and it changes the former practice of pleading to the alternative writ of mandamus.

R.M.A. ex rel. Appleberry v. Blue Springs R-IV School District, 477 S.W.3d 185 (Mo. App. W.D. 2015)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 2015-12-08 · WD 78535 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing Rule 94.04, Rule 94.05, Rule 94.07 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Rule 94.04 provides that "[i]f the court is of the opinion that the preliminary order in mandamus should be granted, such order shall be issued.” If a preliminary order is issued, Rule 94.05 provides that ‘‘[t]he preliminary order shall order the respondent to file an answer within the time fixed by the order,” and "[t]he clerk shall... deliver a copy of the preliminary order, together with a copy of the petition, for service.... [which] may be made as provided in Rule 54.”
Rule 94.07 requires that “[t]he answer [ordered by Rule 94.05] shall be directed to the petition in mandamus.... [and] may include or be accompanied by one or more motions."
