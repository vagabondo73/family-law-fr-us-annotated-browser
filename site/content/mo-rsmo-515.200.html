515.200 — Exceptions to report.

in forceIn force since : 1939-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=515.200 · Interactive browser

Current text

All exceptions to the report of the referees shall be in writing, and filed within four days, in term, after the report is filed, and shall be argued without delay.
