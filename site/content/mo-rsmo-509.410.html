509.410 — Objection that no demand has been made not available, when.

in forceIn force since : 1939-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=509.410 · Interactive browser

Current text

It shall not hereafter be available to a party as an objection that no demand for the subject matter of a suit was made prior to its institution, unless it is expressly set up by way of defense in the answer or replication, and is also accompanied with a tender of the amount that is due; in which case, if the plaintiff will further prosecute his suit, and shall not recover a greater sum than is tendered, he shall pay all costs. This provision shall be applicable as well to actions for property as for money; when property is tendered the damages for its detention, if any, shall also be tendered.

Moran v. Hubbartt, 178 S.W.3d 604 (Mo. App. W.D. 2005)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 2005-09-27 · No. WD 64419 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing Rule 55.31, § 509.410 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

In particular, the Hubbartts contend that to prevail in an action brought under quantum meruit, Mr. Moran was required to plead and prove, not only that they failed to pay the money allegedly owed Mr. Moran, but also that Mr. Moran made a prior demand for the amount owed. Under Rule 55.31: A party cannot object that no demand for the subject matter of a civil action was made prior to its institution unless it is expressly set up by way of defense in the answer or reply and is also accompanied with a tender of the amount or thing that is due[.] See also Section 509.410, RSMo 2000.
They also made no tender of the amount allegedly due as required by Rule 55.31.
Express Co., 135 Mo.App. 330, 116 S.W. 9, 11 (1909) (discussing section 1575, RSMo 1899, the predecessor statute to section 509.410).
