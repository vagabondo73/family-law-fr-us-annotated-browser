516.200 — If defendant be out of state before or departs after cause of action commences, when action may be commenced.

in forceIn force since : 2009-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=516.200 · Interactive browser

Current text

If at any time when any cause of action herein specified accrues against any person who is a resident of this state, and he is absent therefrom, such action may be commenced within the times herein respectively limited, after the return of such person into the state.

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing Rule 55.33, Rule 54.01, Rule 54.21, § 516.200 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Rule 55.33(c)(2) requires that, within the time allowed for service, Richard “knew or should have known that, but for a mistake ..., the action would have been brought against [him].”
The time allowed for service may extend beyond the statute of limitations period for filing the lawsuit but only to the extent described in Rule 54.01(c), which requires that service be made “promptly.”
No familiarity or disrespect is intended. . The fact that a period of less than thirty days is within the time allowed for service is confirmed by Rule 54.21, which provides: The officer or other person receiving a summons or other process shall serve the same and make return of service promptly.
