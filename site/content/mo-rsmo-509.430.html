509.430 — Counterclaim exceeding opposing claim.

in forceIn force since : 1943-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=509.430 · Interactive browser

Current text

A counterclaim may or may not diminish or defeat the recovery sought by the opposing party. It may claim relief exceeding in amount or different in kind from that sought in the pleading of the opposing party.

Correspondences (CPC ↔ Missouri)

art. 37 · Légifrance55.32, 509.430partial13Article 37 allows the court to hear certain smaller incidental and counterclaims despite their aggregate exceeding its jurisdictional threshold. The Missouri texts regulate counterclaims exceeding the opposing claim, but do not state that aggregate-amount rule or any certification procedure.
art. 38 · Légifrance55.32, 509.430partial13Article 38 provides alternatives when an incidental claim exceeds jurisdiction and an exception for damages counterclaims based exclusively on the original claim. The Missouri texts only permit counterclaims exceeding the opposing claim; they do not provide those alternatives or exception.
art. 64 · Légifrance55.32, 509.430equivalent13The article defines a counterclaim as a defendant’s claim seeking relief beyond simple dismissal. Rule 55.32(c) and RSMo 509.430 likewise provide that a counterclaim may exceed the opposing claim or seek relief of a different kind, rather than merely defeat it.

Summary (non-official) : St. Louis Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing § 509.430 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Section 509.430, upon which the respondent relies, permits the defendant in a civil suit to set forth as a counterclaim matters “different in kind from that sought in the pleading of the opposing party”.
This motion was overruled and the case was set for trial. The relator then sought prohibition and our preliminary writ was issued. It is the respondent’s contention that under Section 509.430 RSMo 1949, V.A.M.S., and Supreme Court Rule 3.16, 42 V.A.M.S., the defendant may file a cross bill for divorce in an action brought under 'Chapter 454.
