85.01 — Definitions

in forceIn force since : 1981-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199808 · Rule 85 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

As used in this Rule 85 "claimant" means the party seeking the attachment, "owner" means the party against whose property the attachment is sought, and "nonowner" means a nonowner from whose possession the property is taken.

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is new.

Downing v. Howe, 60 S.W.3d 646 (Mo. App. 2001)

Missouri Court of Appeals · 2001-09-14 · No. 24098 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing Rule 74.06, Rule 85.01 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

The Supreme Court of Missouri has held that there is a higher standard invoked to set aside a judgment on the basis of Rule 74.06 than on the basis of either Rule 75.01 or 74.05.
SHRUM, P.J., and MONTGOMERY, J„ concur. . All rule references are to Missouri Court Rules (2001). . Rule 85.01(a)(1) states, "[a] judgment becomes final at the expiration of thirty days after its entry if no timely authorized after-trial motion is filed." . Section 452.423.1 provides, in pertinent part: The court shall appoint a guardian ad litem in any proceeding in which child abuse or neglect is alleged. See Chapin v. Chapin, 985 S.W.2d 897, 901 n. 1 (Mo.App. 1999) for discussion of the definition of the terms "abuse” and "neglect.” . Rule 74.06(b) reads, in pertinent part: On motion and upon such terms as are just, the court may relieve a party or his legal representative from a final judgment or order for the following reasons: (4) the judgement is void....
“A Rule 74.06(b) motion to declare a judgment void is only appropriate when the court which rendered that judgment lacked jurisdiction over the subject matter or the parties, or acted in a manner inconsistent with due process of law.”
