95.04 — Order of Change of Name – When

in forceIn force since : 1981-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199936 · Interactive browser

Current text

The court shall order the desired change of name if it finds that the change would be proper and not detrimental to the interests of any other person.

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is substantially the same as that portion of prior Rule 95.01 following the semi-colon.

B_L_W_ ex rel. Ellen K_ v. Wollweber, 823 S.W.2d 119 (Mo. App. S.D. 1992)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Southern District · 1992-01-21 · No. 17422 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Southern District opinion applying or construing 95.03, 95.04 (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

Rule 95.03 reads, in part: "When a minor files a petition for change of name the written consent of each known parent shall be filed.
If the written consent of each known parent is not filed, a copy of the petition together with a notice stating the date of the hearing shall be served on each known parent not consent-ing_" Rule 95.04 reads: “The court shall order the desired change of name if it finds that the change would be proper and not detrimental to the interests of any other person.”
A trial court has discretion under Rules 95.03 and 95.04 to deny a petition if there is evidence that the change would be detrimental to the interests of other persons.

Brown v. Wyrick, 626 S.W.2d 674 (Mo. App. W.D. 1981)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 1981-12-29 · Nos. WD 31799, WD 31800 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing Rule 95.01, Rule 95.04 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

The judgment of the trial court was reversed on appeal, and the appellate court’s reasons for doing so were summarized as follows: “The trial judge retains the discretion under Rule 95.01, supra, to deny a petition if he has before him some evidence that third parties will be harmed by the name change.
Petitioners imply that under Rule 95.01 (present Rule 95.04) the trial court was exclusively burdened to sua sponte ferret out potential detriment to third persons in change of name proceedings.
Consequently, in the context of these appeals, the italized portion of Rule 95.01 is a parameter to be considered in determining whether intervenors had a right to intervene pursuant to Rule 52.12(a)(2).
