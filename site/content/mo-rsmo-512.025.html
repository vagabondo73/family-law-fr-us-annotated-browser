512.025 — Habeas corpus proceedings for custody of minor child, who may appeal.

in forceIn force since : 1986-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=512.025 · Interactive browser

Current text

Any party may appeal to the court of appeals from a decision in a habeas corpus proceeding involving the custody of a minor child where there is in effect, at the time of the hearing on the writ, no prior court order determining custody. Such appeal shall be expedited by the court in every manner practicable.

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District opinion applying or construing § 512.025 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Section 512.025 provides for an appeal from a habeas corpus proceeding in child custody if no prior couH order detemining custody is in effect.
Accordingly, because there is a prior court order determining the legal custody of A.T., Petitioner may not appeal the probate court’s decision in her habeas corpus proceeding via Section 512.025.
However, because Petitioner is appealing from the dismissal of a writ of habeas corpus in a child custody matter, we review Section 512.025.
