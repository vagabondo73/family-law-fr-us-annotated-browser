84.28 — Execution or Remand by Appellate Court – Costs

in forceIn force since : 1980-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199805 · Rule 84 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

The court, upon the determination of any cause on appeal, may award execution to carry the same into effect, or may remand the cause with its decision therein, including any judgment or order as to costs made by the appellate court, to the court from which the cause was appealed, and such determination shall be carried into execution by that court.

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District opinion applying or construing Rule 74.16, Rule 84.28 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

We certainly would agree that it is best practice for an attorney to submit an affidavit in support of their Rule 74.16 motion for fees that competently and thoroughly attests to the legal work done and fees accrued, but Appellants direct us to no rule of law requiring any specific quantum of evidence, nor is one likely to exist given that a circuit court “may determine attorney fees without the aid of evidence.”
Rule 84.28 2 governs a circuit court’s authority on remand: The court, upon the determination of any cause on appeal, may award execution to carry the same into effect, or may remand the cause with its decision therein, including any judgment or order as to costs made by the appellate court, to the court from which the cause was appealed, and such determination shall be carried into execution by that court.
