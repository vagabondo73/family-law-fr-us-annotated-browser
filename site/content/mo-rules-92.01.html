92.01 — Who May Grant

in forceIn force since : 2006-07-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199918 · Interactive browser

Current text

Injunctions may be granted by a circuit or associate circuit judge.

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is substantially the same as Sections 526.010 and 526.020, RSMo.
