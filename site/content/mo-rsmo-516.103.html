516.103 — No tolling by filing of administrative actions, certain suits.

in forceIn force since : 1993-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=516.103 · Interactive browser

Current text

The time for commencement of any suit provided for in sections 516.380, 516.390 and 516.400, shall not be tolled by the filing or pendency of any administrative complaint or action and no such suit may be brought or maintained unless commenced within the time prescribed by said sections. An administrative order authorizing the commencement of any such suit shall not be considered as evidence of the violations alleged in any such suit.

See also : 516.380 (mo-rsmo), 516.390 (mo-rsmo), 516.400 (mo-rsmo)

Sean A. Brady v. John R. Ashcroft and David M. Minnick, No. WD84470 (Mo. App. W.D. 2022)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 2022-01-18 · WD84470 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing § 516.380 RSMo, § 516.390 RSMo, § 516.400 RSMo, § 516.100 RSMo, Rule 53.01, Rule 51.05 and others (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

While the statute provides that the “pendency of any administrative complaint or action” does not toll the limitations periods specified in §§ 516.380, .390, or .400, the wording of § 516.103 strongly suggests that those limitations periods do not apply to an “administrative complaint or action” itself. 25
Indeed, in Sure-Way, this Court held that the running of the two-year statute of limitations found in § 516.390 was tolled while the Division of Transportation prosecuted an administrative proceeding to obtain authorization to file the judicial civil-penalty action.
Section 516.103 provides: The time for commencement of any suit provided for in sections 516.380, 516.390 and 516.400, shall not be tolled by the filing or pendency of any administrative complaint or action and no such suit may be brought or maintained unless commenced within the time prescribed by said sections.

State ex rel. Missouri Division of Transportation v. Sure-Way Transportation, Inc., 884 S.W.2d 349 (Mo. App. W.D. 1994)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 1994-08-30 · No. WD 48622 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing § 516.103 RSMo, § 516.390 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Section 516.103 means what it says: an administrative law judge’s decision authorizing the Division’s general counsel to file a penalty action is not conclusive to the issue of whether the carrier has violated the law and is subject to penalty.
Sure-Way defended the process as a "weeding out” process, but we fail to understand why these cases, contrary to any other lawsuits initiated in circuit courts, need a special "weeding out" step. As we pointed out in Sure-Way I, 836 S.W.2d at 27, § 516.390 does not contemplate this special process; it is mandated by the judiciary.
Moreover, on August 28, 1993, § 516.103, RSMo Cum.Supp.1993, became effective.
