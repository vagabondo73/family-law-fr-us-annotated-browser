45.01 — Court Always Open for Certain Purposes

in forceIn force since : 1972-09-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199566 · Interactive browser

Current text

The courts shall be deemed always open for the purpose of filing any pleading or other proper paper, of issuing and returning mesne and final process, and of making and directing all interlocutory motions, orders and rules.

Issues : proc.mo.general.courts-clerks

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing Rule 45.01 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Rule 45.01 states that courts are “always open for the purpose of filing any pleading....” There is no allegation that counsel attempted to contact a member of the clerk’s office or a judge by telephone to arrange alternative filing procedures.
