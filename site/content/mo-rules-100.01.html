100.01 — Review in Circuit Court

in forceIn force since : 1983-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=200003 · Interactive browser

Current text

The provisions of sections 536.100 through 536.150, RSMo, shall govern procedure in circuit courts for judicial review of actions of administrative agencies unless the statute governing a particular agency contains different provisions for such review.

Rule 100.01 states: “The provisions of sections 536.100 through 536.150, RSMo, shall govern procedure in circuit courts for judicial review of actions of administrative agencies unless the statute governing a particular agency contains different provisions for such review.”
In this point relator also states that the motion should have been sustained under Rule 55.27(a)(10) because there was “another action pending between the same parties for the same cause in this state”.
The opinion stated that the cases supporting that general rule “which was applicable prior to adoption of amended Rule 53.01 [effective December 1, 1972] should no longer be followed.” 521 S.W.2d at 395.
