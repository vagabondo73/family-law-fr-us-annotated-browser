Summary rule

“Rule 54.06(b) mandates compliance with the minimum contacts test by requiring that one whom the state would subject to an in personam judgment for maintenance, support, attorney’s fees, suit money, or disposition of marital property must have “lived in lawful marriage within this state.”” (Crouch v. Crouch, Mo. banc 1982; followed in Patel v. Patel, 2012, and Wilson v. Wilson, 2023). Due process requires that a nonresident defendant “have certain minimum contacts with it such that the maintenance of the suit does not offend “traditional notions of fair play and substantial justice.”” (Crouch).
