97.02 — Form of Action – Parties

in forceIn force since : 1983-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199973 · Rule 97 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

Proceedings in prohibition shall be by a civil action in which the person seeking relief is relator and the person against whom such relief is sought is respondent. If appropriate, there may be multiple relators or multiple respondents.

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is substantially the same as prior Rule 97.01.

See also : 97.01 (mo-rules)
