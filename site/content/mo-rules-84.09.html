84.09 — Appeal – Dismissal in Appellate Court

in forceIn force since : 1994-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199786 · Rule 84 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

An appellant may file a dismissal of the appellant's appeal in the appellate court at any time prior to the submission of the cause in the appellate court.

art. 400 · Légifrance84.09partial—Withdrawal of an appeal or opposition is generally allowed in all matters unless otherwise provided. Rule 84.09 permits only an appellant to file dismissal of an appeal before submission, and does not address opposition or establish the same general rule.
art. 403 · Légifrance84.09partial—Withdrawal of an appeal entails acquiescence and is ineffective if another party later files a regular appeal. Rule 84.09 permits an appellant to dismiss an appeal before submission, but does not provide those effects or the later-appeal condition.
art. 405 · Légifrance84.09partial—This article applies other rules to withdrawal of an appeal or opposition without stating their content. Rule 84.09 partially addresses dismissal of an appeal, but no supplied Missouri provision covers the full incorporation rule.
art. 621 · Légifrance81.04, 84.08, 84.09partial—Article 621 generally bars a second cassation appeal after rejection, inadmissibility, or forfeiture, and bars a respondent who failed to take a timely cross-appeal from later appealing as of right. Rule 81.04(c) governs cross-appeal timing, while Rules 84.08 and 84.09 address dismissal or withdrawal without the same general bar.
art. 1024 · Légifrance84.09partial—Article 1024 requires acceptance of an appeal withdrawal when it contains reservations or when a cross-appeal was previously filed. Rule 84.09 directly addresses appellate dismissal but does not impose those acceptance conditions; Rule 67.02 provides consent requirements for some civil dismissals, not specifically appeals.
art. 1025 · Légifrance84.09partial—Article 1025 applies several dismissal provisions to withdrawal of an appeal. Rule 84.09 directly governs appellate dismissal and Rule 67.02 voluntary dismissal of a civil action, but neither reproduces the specific cross-application mechanism in the French article.
art. 1026 · Légifrance84.09partial—Article 1026 specifies the judicial officer and form for recording withdrawal, permits a ruling on article 700 requests, and treats a later judgment as an affirmance-like disposition in certain circumstances. The Missouri rules address appellate dismissal, disposition, written decisions, and costs, but not this specific French procedure.

In re Forbeck, 310 S.W.3d 740 (Mo. App. E.D. 2010)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District · 2010-05-18 · No. ED 93195 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District opinion applying or construing Rule 84.09, Rule 67.01, Rule 67.02 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Rule 84.09 allows an appellant to file a dismissal of his appeal with this Court at any time prior to the submission of the cause.
In most instances, when a cause is dismissed without prejudice, a party may file another action in the same court; thus, such a dismissal is not a final judgment for the purpose of appeal. Estate of Johnson, 912 S.W.2d 560, 561 (Mo.App. E.D.1995); Matter of Myhre, 772 S.W.2d at 416 (when question of whether evidence presented in support of petition for declaration of incapacity and for appointment of guardian is sufficient to make fact issue of alleged incapacity is not adjudicated before dismissal, petition may be refiled and cause retried upon same or new evidence with no effect on result because of prior dismissal); Rule 67.01.
We find, however, that Rule 67.02(b), providing for the voluntary dismissal of an action at the plaintiffs instance “upon order of the court upon such terms and conditions as the court deems proper [,]” is applicable here and requires us to dismiss the appeals for lack of an appealable judgment.

Supreme Court of Missouri · 1990-09-11 · No. 72354 published

Justification : The opinion (1990-09-11) predates the current version of Rule 84.09 (in force since 1994-01-01) but quotes the language it applies — “at any time prior to the submission of the cause in the appellate court.” — and that language appears verbatim (normalized for punctuation/case) in the current text; the interpreted language is therefore identical in wording and function.

Summary (non-official) : Supreme Court of Missouri opinion applying or construing Rule 84.09, Rule 75.01 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

McMullin v. Satz is misplaced. Satz turns on an interpretation of Rule 84.09; that rule permits an appellant to dismiss an appeal “at any time prior to the submission of the cause in the appellate court.”
Rule 75.01 empowers the trial court to retain control over judgments “during the thirty-day period after entry of judgment” and permits the trial court to “vacate, reopen, correct, amend, or modify its judgment within that time” for good cause.
Because Bell is not an appellant, but is instead the applicant for a writ of certiorari or review, Rule 84.09 does not apply.
