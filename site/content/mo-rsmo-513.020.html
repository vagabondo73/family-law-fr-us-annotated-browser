513.020 — Executions may issue, when.

in forceIn force since : 1939-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=513.020 · Interactive browser

Current text

Executions may issue upon a judgment at any time within ten years after the rendition of such judgment.

Correspondences (CPC ↔ Missouri)

CPCMissouriEquivalenceFRCPNote
art. 501 · Légifrance81.09, 513.015, 513.020partial—Article 501 makes a judgment executable upon finality, subject to a grace period or provisional enforcement. Rule 81.09 governs stays during appeal, while §§ 513.015 and 513.020 authorize and time-limit executions, but do not impose the same general finality condition.

Keipp v. Keipp, 385 S.W.3d 470 (Mo. App. W.D. 2012)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 2012-07-31 · No. WD 74272 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing § 513.020 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Ms. Gruer is correct in stating that section 513.020 provides that “[e]xecutions may issue upon a judgment at any time within ten years after the rendition of such judgment,” but here, the execution of the Judgment referred to in the statute consists of enforcement of the incorporated Agreement in accordance with its terms.
Because the Agreement is not ambiguous and the issue of conscionability was not proper for the trial court to reconsider, Ms. Gruer’s second point on appeal is denied. How Statute Governs Execution of the Judgment Ms. Gruer argues in her first point on appeal that the trial court erred in granting Mr. Keipp’s motion to quash garnishment because section 513.020 states that “[executions may issue upon a judgment at any time within ten years after the rendition of such judgment.”
