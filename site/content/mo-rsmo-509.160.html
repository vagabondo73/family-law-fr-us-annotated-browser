509.160 — Particularity required in all averments of fraud or mistake.

in forceIn force since : 1943-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=509.160 · Interactive browser

Current text

In all averments of fraud or mistake, the circumstances constituting fraud or mistake shall be stated with particularity. Malice, intent, knowledge, and other condition of mind of a person may be averred generally.

Summary (non-official) : Supreme Court of Missouri opinion applying or construing § 509.160 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

While fraud is not pleaded with the particularity required by § 509.160, the respondents are deemed to have waived objections to the petition by failing to require a more definite statement or a bill of particulars.
