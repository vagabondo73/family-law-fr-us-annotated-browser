52.06 — Misjoinder and Nonjoinder of Parties

in forceIn force since : 1972-12-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199598 · Rule 52 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

Misjoinder of parties is not ground for dismissal of an action. Parties may be dropped or added by order of the court on motion of any party or of its own initiative at any stage of the action and on such terms as are just. Any claim against a party may be severed and proceeded with separately.
