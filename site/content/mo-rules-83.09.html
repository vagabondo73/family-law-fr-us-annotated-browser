83.09 — Determination of Cases Following Transfer

in forceIn force since : 1972-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199773 · Rule 83 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

Any case coming to this Court from a district of the Court of Appeals, whether by certification, transfer or certiorari, may be finally determined the same as on original appeal. If, however, in cases transferred on order of this Court, the Court concludes that the transfer was improvidently granted, the case may be retransferred to the Court of Appeals.

Williams v. Hubbard, 455 S.W.3d 426 (Mo. 2015)

Supreme Court of Missouri · 2015-02-03 · No. SC 93853 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Supreme Court of Missouri opinion applying or construing Rule 83.08, Rule 84.04, Rule 83.09, Rule 83.03, Rule 74.04 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Rule 83.08(b) provides that a “party may file a substitute brief in this Court.”
If a party chooses to file a substitute brief, however, Rule 88.08(b) provides that the “substitute brief shall conform with Rule 84.04, [that it] shall include all claims the party desires this Court to review, [and that it] shall not alter the basis of any claim that was raised in the court of appeals brief[.]” Finally, Rule 83.08(a) provides that the parties are to retain the same appellant or respondent designations they had before the transfer.
Rule 83.09 provides that, unless the matter is retransferred, every appeal that comes to this Court from the court of appeals — whether by certification, transfer (before or after opinion), or certiorari — will be decided “the same as on original appeal.” This means that the transfer immediately renders the court of appeals’ decision (if any) a nullity and the parties must proceed in this Court as though the appeal properly had been filed here in the first instance.
