525.110 — Execution same as on general judgment.

in forceIn force since : 1939-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=525.110 · Interactive browser

Current text

When judgment is rendered against any garnishee, the execution shall be such as is allowed by law on general judgment.

Issues : proc.mo.enforcement.garnishment

Central Missouri Paving Co. v. Kraft, 678 S.W.2d 420 (Mo. App. W.D. 1984)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 1984-07-31 · No. WD 34587 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing § 525.190 RSMo, § 525.110 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Section 525.190 RSMo 1949, V.A.M.S. Not only is the judgment in a garnishment proceeding a “final judgment” in that it finally disposes of all the issues and parties, but when that judgment is in favor of the party seeking to enforce his judgment by way of garnishment, it is one upon which execution “such as is allowed by law on general judgment” may issue to enforce said judgment.
Section 525.110 RSMo 1949, V.A.M.S. The orders quashing the writs of garnishment were final judgments within the meaning of Section 512.020 RSMo 1949, V.A.M.S., which disposed of the issues between the appellant as plaintiff in the garnishment proceeding and the garnishees.
