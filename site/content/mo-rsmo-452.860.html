452.860 — Enforcement of registered determination.

in forceIn force since : 2009-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=452.860 · Interactive browser

Current text

1. A court of this state may grant any relief normally available under the provisions of the laws of this state to enforce a registered child custody determination made by a court of another state.

2. A court of this state shall recognize and enforce, but shall not modify, except in accordance with sections 452.740 to 452.845, a registered child custody determination of another state.

Section 452.740 of the UCCJEA “governs initial child custody determinations and prioritizes home state jurisdiction over all other jurisdictional bases.”
Section 452.745.1 “provides that a court [such as the Missouri court] that has made 7 “Missouri courts interpret the UCCJEA jurisdictional provisions to dictate whether a Missouri court has the statutory authority to grant relief in a particular matter, not whether a Missouri court has subject matter jurisdiction.”
“The jurisdictional provisions of sections 452.745 and 452.770 apply to ‘child custody determination[s].’” Id. at 830.
