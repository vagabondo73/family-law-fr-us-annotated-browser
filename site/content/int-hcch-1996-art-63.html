63 — Article 63

in forceIn force since : 2002-01-01 · Applicable : FR

Official source : https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/full-text/?cid=70 · Status table (HCCH) · Interactive browser

Current text

The depositary shall notify the States Members of the Hague Conference on Private International Law and the States which have acceded in accordance with Article 58 of the following - a) the signatures, ratifications, acceptances and approvals referred to in Article 57; b) the accessions and objections raised to accessions referred to in Article 58; c) the date on which the Convention enters into force in accordance with Article 61; d) the declarations referred to in Articles 34, paragraph 2, and 59; e) the agreements referred to in Article 39; f) the reservations referred to in Articles 54, paragraph 2, and 55 and the withdrawals referred to in Article 60, paragraph 2; g) the denunciations referred to in Article 62. In witness whereof the undersigned, being duly authorised thereto, have signed this Convention. Done at The Hague, on the 19th day of October 1996, in the English and French languages, both texts being equally authentic, in a single copy which shall be deposited in the archives of the Government of the Kingdom of the Netherlands, and of which a certified copy shall be sent, through diplomatic channels, to each of the States Members of the Hague Conference on Private International Law at the date of its Eighteenth Session.
