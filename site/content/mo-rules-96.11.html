96.11 — Court May Order Sale Without Appointment of Commissioners

in forceIn force since : 1981-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199949 · Rule 96 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

If the court finds that partition in kind cannot be made without great prejudice to the owners an order of sale may be made without the appointment of commissioners.

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is substantially the same as that portion of prior Rule 96.53 which precedes the first semi-colon.

In part, Rule 96.01 provides that "[o]wners of interests in land in ... tenancy in common ... may bring an action for partition, if the same can be done without great prejudice to the parties in interest, and if not, then for a sale of the property, and a division of the proceeds thereof.”
Rule 96.11 provides that "[i]f the court finds that partition in kind cannot be made without great prejudice to the owners an order of sale may be made without the appointment of commissioners.” . Plaintiffs’ petition had two counts, the first seeking partition of the 120 acres described in the judgment, and the second praying for partition of lots situated in Prosperity, Missouri.
