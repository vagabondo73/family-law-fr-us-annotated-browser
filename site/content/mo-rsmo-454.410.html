454.410 — Assignment of support rights, obligation to state, when.

in forceIn force since : 1997-07-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=454.410 · Interactive browser

Current text

Support rights assigned to the state shall constitute an obligation owed to the state by the person responsible for providing such support and the obligation shall be collectible pursuant to all legal processes.

Summary (non-official) : Supreme Court of Missouri opinion applying or construing § 454.460, § 454.470, § 454.475, § 454.425, § 454.410, 83.03 RSMo (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

In some cases, parental support is not provided (or not adequate) and the state provides “public assistance” (as defined by section 454.460(12)) to help support the child.
If there is no existing support order, FSD may issue a “notice and finding of financial responsibility” (“NFFR”) pursuant to section 454.470.
If either parent raises a timely objection that requires evidence to resolve, “a hearing shall be held in the manner provided by section 454.475.
Missouri complies with this requirement through section 454.425, which provides that FSD “shall render child support services authorized pursuant to this chapter to persons who are not recipients of public assistance as well as to such recipients.”
