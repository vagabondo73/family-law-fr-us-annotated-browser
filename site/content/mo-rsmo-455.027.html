455.027 — No filing fee, court cost, or bond shall be required.

in forceIn force since : 2011-08-28 · Applicable : MO

No filing fees, court costs, or bond shall be assessed to the petitioner in an action commenced pursuant to sections 455.010 to 455.090.

Binding interpretations (2)

Afridi v. Auman, 301 S.W.3d 595 (Mo. App. W.D. 2010)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 2010-02-02 · No. WD 70090 published
Basis (b): identical text — functional-review

Justification : Functional review: the opinion (2010-02-02) applied § 455.027 RSMo as in force from 2003-08-28 (revisor bid 52785); current text in force since 2011-08-28. Interpreted subsection(s) identified from the opinion: whole. Compared subsection whole: version text “No filing fees, court costs, or bond shall be assessed to the petitioner in an action commenced pursuant to sections 455.010 to 455.085.” / current text “No filing fees, court costs, or bond shall be assessed to the petitioner in an action commenced pursuant to sections 455.010 to 455.090.”. The opinion interprets the operative language of § 455.027 stating that no filing fees, court costs, or bond may be assessed to the petitioner. That language is unchanged; only the cross-reference to §§ 455.010–455.085 was updated to §§ 455.010–455.090, without altering the interpreted rule.

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing 77.01, § 455.027 RSMo (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

In support of his motion and in support of this appeal, Auman cites Rule 77.01, which states: In civil actions, the party prevailing shall recover his costs against the other party, unless otherwise provided in these rules or by law.
At section 455.027, RSMo 2003, it is stated: No filing fees, court costs, or bond shall be assessed to the petitioner in an action commenced pursuant to sections 455.010 to 455.085.
(Emphasis added.) As stated previously, Auman relies upon Rule 77.01 for his argument that “costs” must be awarded to him.
Section 455.027 can safely be said to “otherwise ... provide[ ] by law” that “costs” are not recoverable in this setting.

In the Interest of G.F., 276 S.W.3d 327 (Mo. App. E.D. 2009)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District · 2009-01-13 · No. ED 90104 published
Basis (b): identical text — text-identical

Justification : The opinion (2009-01-13) predates the current version of § 455.027 RSMo (in force since 2011-08-28) but quotes the statutory/rule language it interprets — “no filing fees, court costs, or bond shall be assessed to the petitioner” — and that language appears verbatim in the current text and in the version in force at the date of the opinion; the interpreted language is therefore identical in wording and function.

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District opinion applying or construing § 455.504, § 455.500, § 455.027 RSMo (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

In this proceeding for an order of child protection, we must decide two novel issues relative to exemptions sought by a petitioner under Section 455.504.2 of the Child Protection Orders Act.
Discussion The relevant statute, Section 455.504.2 RSMo 2000, provides: No filing fees, court costs, or bond shall be assessed to the petitioner in an action commenced under sections 455.500 to 455.538.
Critically, when the legislature amended Section 455.027, the provision relating to costs in an order-of-adult-protection proceedings, it added identical language to amend Section 455.504.2, the provision at issue in this case, which relates to costs in an order-of-child-protection proceedings.
The petitioner argued she should be allowed to proceed without payment of such expenses because of the statutory exemption found in Section 455.504.2.
