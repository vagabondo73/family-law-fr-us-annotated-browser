516.145 — What actions within one year.

in forceIn force since : 1990-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=516.145 · Interactive browser

Current text

Within one year: all actions brought by an offender, as defined in section 217.010, against the department of corrections or any entity or division thereof, or any employee or former employee for an act in an official capacity, or by the omission of an official duty.
