454.1563 — Inappropriate tribunal.

in forceIn force since : 2011-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=454.1563 · Interactive browser

Current text

If a petition or comparable pleading is received by an inappropriate tribunal of this state, the tribunal shall forward the pleading and accompanying documents to an appropriate tribunal of this state or another state and notify the petitioner where and when the pleading was sent.

Issues : mo.uifsa.procedure

Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District opinion applying or construing § 454.1563, § 454.1533, § 454.1500, § 454.1527, § 454.1662, § 454.1638 RSMo (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

Section 454.1563 states: "If a petition or comparable pleading is received by an inappropriate tribunal of this state, the tribunal shall forward the pleading and accompanying documents to an appropriate tribunal of this state or another state and notify the petitioner where and when the pleading was sent."
Section 454.1533(c) provides that if two or more child support orders have been issued for the same obligor and same child, upon request of a party, a tribunal of this state having personal jurisdiction over both the obligor and the obligee shall determine which order controls.
The UIFSA, codified in Missouri at Sections 454.1500 to 454.1730, controls the establishment, enforcement, or modification of support orders across state lines.
See Section 454.1527(c)("[i]f a tribunal of another state has issued a child support order pursuant to the UIFSA ..., tribunals of [Missouri] shall recognize the continuing, exclusive jurisdiction of the tribunal of the other state").
