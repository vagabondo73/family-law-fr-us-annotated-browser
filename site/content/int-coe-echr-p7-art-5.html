Council of Europe › Protocol No. 7 to the Convention for the Protection of Human Rights and Fundamental Freedoms (ETS No. 117)

5 — Equality between spouses

in forceIn force since : 1988-11-01 · Applicable : FR

Official source : https://www.coe.int/en/web/conventions/full-list?module=signatures-by-treaty&treatynum=117 · Text (Council of Europe) · Interactive browser

Current text

Spouses shall enjoy equality of rights and responsibilities of a private law character between them, and in their relations with their children, as to marriage, during marriage and in the event of its dissolution. This Article shall not prevent States from taking such measures as are necessary in the interests of the children.
