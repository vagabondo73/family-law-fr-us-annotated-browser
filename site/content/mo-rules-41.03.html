41.03 — Rules – Construction

in forceIn force since : 1972-09-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199551 · Rule 41 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

Rules 41 to 101, inclusive, shall be construed to secure the just, speedy and inexpensive determination of every action.

However, the plain language of Rule 77.04, includes language that is mandatory: At any time more than thirty days before the trial begins, a party defending against a claim may serve upon the adverse party an offer to allow judgment to be taken against the defending party for the money or property or to the effect specified in the offer, with costs then accrued. While we recognize that Rule 41.03 mandates that we construe the Supreme Court Rules to provide a “just, speedy and inexpensive determination of every action, we cannot ignore the explicit language of Rules 52.13 and 44.01 and our lack of inherent power to substitute a party after death.
