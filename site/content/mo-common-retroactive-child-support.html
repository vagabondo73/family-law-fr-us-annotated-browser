retroactive-child-support — Retroactive child support — limits

in forceIn force since : 2019-05-07 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/file.jsp?id=141133 · Leading source: Lueckenotte v. Lueckenotte, 34 S.W.3d 387 (Mo. banc 2001) · Leading source: Christine Ann Lollar v. Summary rule

“The trial court should also determine whether to make the order of child support retroactive to the date of the filing of the motion to modify child support and personal service.” (Lueckenotte v. Lueckenotte, Mo. banc 2001). “Although trial courts have discretion under Section 452.340.1 to award retroactive child support, that discretion extends only to the date the petition for dissolution was filed, and Missouri courts have determined that an award predating that filing constitutes an abuse of discretion.” (Lollar, 2019). Section 452.340.1 “permits but does not require an order of retroactive child support from the date of the filing of the petition.” (Hanson, 2018).

Rule sources : Lueckenotte v. Lueckenotte, 34 S.W.3d 387 (Mo. banc 2001), Christine Ann Lollar v. Richard Dwain Lollar, No. ED106488 (Mo. App. E.D. 2019), Hanson v. Hanson (In re Hanson), 540 S.W.3d 512 (Mo. App. S.D. 2018)
