510.262 — Applicability of certain statute changes for cases filed after August 28, 2020.

in forceIn force since : 2020-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=510.262 · Interactive browser

Current text

The provisions of this act* shall apply to causes of action filed on or after August 28, 2020.
