210.850 — Uniformity of application and construction.

in forceIn force since : 1987-07-15 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=210.850 · Interactive browser

Current text

The provisions of sections 210.817 to 210.852 shall be applied and construed to effectuate its general purpose to make uniform the law with respect to the subject of sections 210.817 to 210.852 among the states enacting it.

Snead ex rel. Snead v. Cordes ex rel. Golding, 811 S.W.2d 391 (Mo. App. W.D. 1991)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 1991-04-23 · No. WD 43537 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing 74.06, § 210.850 RSMo (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

Rule 74.06(b) provides that a final judgment may be set aside for the following reasons: (1) Mistake, inadvertence, surprise or excusable neglect; (2) Fraud (whether heretofore denominated intrinsic or extrinsic), misrepresentation or other misconduct of an adverse party; (3) The judgment is irregular; (4) The judgment is void; or (5) The judgment has been satisfied, released or discharged, or a prior judgment upon which it is based has been reversed or otherwise vacated, or it is no longer equitable that the judgment remain in force.
However, § 210.850, RSMo Supp.1990, now provides that the provisions of the UPA are to be applied and construed with the purpose of making uniform the law in respect to paternity.
Any settlement approved without notice to parties known to claim to be among the class entitled to bring an action for wrongful death is subject to being set aside under the terms of Rule 74.06(b).
