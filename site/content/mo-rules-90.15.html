90.15 — Garnishment of Wages – Notice of Wage Exemption Statute Required

in forceIn force since : 2016-07-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199884 · Rule 90 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

No notice, summons, or writ of garnishment issued or served under sections 525.010 to 525.310, RSMo, shall attach or purport to attach any wages in excess of the amounts prescribed in subsection 2 of section 525.030, or any applicable federal law, and each such notice, summons, or writ shall have clearly and legibly reproduced thereon the provisions of subsections 2, 5 and 6 of section 525.030, RSMo.

See also : 525.010 (mo-rsmo), 525.030 (mo-rsmo)

Issues : proc.mo.enforcement.garnishment

Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District · 2013-08-06 · No. ED 99158 published
Basis (b): identical text — text-identical

Justification : The opinion (2013-08-06) predates the current version of Rule 90.15 (in force since 2016-07-01) but quotes the language it applies — “any wages in excess of the amounts prescribed in subsection 2 of section 525.030.” — and that language appears verbatim (normalized for punctuation/case) in the current text; the interpreted language is therefore identical in wording and function.

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District opinion applying or construing § 525.030 RSMo, Rule 90.15, Rule 84.21, § 525.010 RSMo, Rule 76.07, § 513.445 RSMo and others (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Neither section 525.030 nor Rule 90.15 requires a garnishee to obtain written verification of head of family exemption status.
While this appeal was pending, Employer filed a motion for attorney’s fees pursuant to Rule 84.21.
We therefore conclude that the legislature did not intend to require a garnishee to obtain written verification of a debtor’s head of family status because section 525.010 distinguishes between a levying officer and garnishee.
