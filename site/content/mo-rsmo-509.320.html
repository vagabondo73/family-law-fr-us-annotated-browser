509.320 — Matter which party may move to strike out.

in forceIn force since : 1943-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=509.320 · Interactive browser

Current text

A party may move to strike any redundant, immaterial, impertinent, or scandalous matter from any pleading.

Issues : proc.mo.pleadings-motions
