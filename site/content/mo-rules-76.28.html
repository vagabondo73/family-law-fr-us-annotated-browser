76.28 — Discovery in Aid of Execution

in forceIn force since : 1999-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=200278 · Rule 76 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

In aid of the judgment or execution, the judgment creditor, or a successor in interest when that interest appears of record, may obtain discovery as provided by Rules 57.01, 57.03, 57.04, 57.05, 57.06, 57.07, 57.09, 57.10, and 58.01 and may enforce discovery as authorized by Rule 61.01 or by an order treating as a contempt of court the failure to comply with the discovery request. However, the scope of post-judgment discovery shall be limited to matters, not privileged, that are relevant to the discovery of assets, including insurance, or income subject to execution or garnishment for the satisfaction of judgments.

See also : 57.01 (mo-rules), 57.03 (mo-rules), 57.04 (mo-rules), 57.05 (mo-rules), 57.06 (mo-rules), 57.07 (mo-rules), 57.09 (mo-rules), 57.10 (mo-rules), 58.01 (mo-rules), 61.01 (mo-rules)

State ex rel. Heidelberg v. Holden, 98 S.W.3d 116 (Mo. App. 2003)

Missouri Court of Appeals · 2003-02-28 · No. 25147 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing Rule 76.28, § 513.380 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

June 28, 2002: pursuant to Rule 76.28, Plaintiff submitted post-judgment interrogatories and requests for production of documents to Relators.
Thus, a judgment debtor examined pursuant to section 513.380, who invokes the privilege against self-incrimination, cannot be compelled to answer a question the answer to which may tend to incriminate him.
After post-judgment discovery was submitted to Relators pursuant to Rule 76.28, Relators asserted their privilege against self-incrimination but Plaintiff failed to present any evidence showing that Rela-tors’ responses would not incriminate them.
