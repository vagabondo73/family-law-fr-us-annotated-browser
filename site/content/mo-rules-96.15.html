96.15 — Commissioners' Report

in forceIn force since : 1981-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199953 · Rule 96 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

The commissioners shall make a written report describing the land divided and the shares allotted to the parties, or that partition cannot be made without great prejudice to the owners, or that partition can be made only of a part of the land. The report shall also list the expenses incurred by the commissioners. It shall be proved or acknowledged in the same manner as deeds are required to be proved or acknowledged to entitle them to be recorded and shall be filed in the office of the clerk of the court.

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is substantially the same as prior Rules 96.25, 96.26, 96.28 and 96.29.

Unsel v. Meier, 880 S.W.2d 646 (Mo. App. 1994)

Missouri Court of Appeals · 1994-08-03 · No. 19343 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing § 512.020 RSMo, Rule 96.15 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

If answered affirmatively, the judgment is appealable under a provision of § 512.020, RSMo 1986, which allows appeals from “interlocutory judgments in actions of partition which determine the rights of the parties.”
However, pursuant to Rule 96.15, the commissioners may report that partition in kind cannot be made without great prejudice to the owners or that partition can be made only of a part of the land.
Otherwise, Defendants’ right to appeal is governed by another provision of § 512.020 which allows an appeal “from any final judgment in the case.”
