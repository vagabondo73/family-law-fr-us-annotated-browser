84.26 — Number of Copies to be Filed in Court of Appeals

in forceIn force since : 1980-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199803 · Rule 84 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

The number of copies of petitions, pleadings, briefs, suggestions, or other papers to be filed in any district of the Court of Appeals shall be as specified in the rules of that district.

Correspondences (CPC ↔ Missouri)

CPCMissouriEquivalenceFRCPNote
art. 994 · Légifrance84.26partial—Article 994 requires memorandum copies based on the number of opposing parties and certification by the signer. Rule 84.26 leaves the number of copies to district rules of the court of appeals and does not provide that formula or require certification.
art. 1031-10 · Légifrance84.26, 81.04partial—The declaration must be filed in copies calculated by reference to the number of respondents, with the clerk recording the filing date by endorsement. Missouri addresses copies and filing or receipt dates, but not this calculation or endorsement requirement.
