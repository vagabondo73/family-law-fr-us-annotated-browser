513.651 — Money acquired by law enforcement agencies, use of funds.

in forceIn force since : 1993-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=513.651 · Interactive browser

Current text

The moneys acquired by law enforcement agencies pursuant to this section and sections 513.647 and 513.649 shall be used only by the law enforcement agency for the investigation or prosecution of criminal activity, the execution of court orders arising from such activity, the enforcement of drug-related crimes, training, drug education, and the safety of both the citizens and law enforcement officers.

See also : 513.647 (mo-rsmo), 513.649 (mo-rsmo)

State v. Gray, 21 S.W.3d 847 (Mo. App. E.D. 2000)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District · 2000-01-11 · No. ED 75493 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District opinion applying or construing § 513.649 RSMo, § 513.651 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

If the court orders a delay in transfer, no later than ten days after the filing of a petition under this section and sections 513.649 and 513.651, a hearing shall be held unless the court deems, for good cause shown, that a continuance should be granted. At the hearing, if the prosecutor has proved by a preponderance of the evidence that the investigation or seizure involved more than one state or that the nature of the investigation or seizure would be better pursued under federal forfeiture statutes, the court shall order that the transfer shall be made.

State v. Sledd, 949 S.W.2d 643 (Mo. App. W.D. 1997)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 1997-07-29 · No. WD 53447 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing § 513.647 RSMo, § 513.617 RSMo, § 513.649 RSMo, § 513.651 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

STATUTORY BACKGROUND OF SECTION 513.647 Mr. Sledd contends that section 513.647 should be construed in the context of the statutory reforms the Missouri legislature enacted in 1993 under Chapter 513 entitled “Missouri Criminal Activities Forfeiture Act” (CAFA), most notably section 513.617.
If the court orders a delay in transfer, no later than ten days after the filing of a petition under this section and sections 513.649 and 513.651, a hearing shall be held unless the court deems, for good cause shown, that a continuance should be granted. At the hearing, if the prosecutor has proved by a preponderance of the evidence that the investigation or seizure involved more than one state or that the nature of the investigation or seizure would be better pursued under the federal forfeiture statutes, the court shall order that the transfer shall be made.
The judgment of the trial court is reversed. STANDARD OF REVIEW This case presents an issue of first impression on the statutory meaning and requirements of section 513.647.
