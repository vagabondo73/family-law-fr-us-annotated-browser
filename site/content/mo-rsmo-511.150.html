511.150 — Assessment of damages.

in forceIn force since : 1939-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=511.150 · Interactive browser

Current text

In all cases of interlocutory judgments not provided for in sections 511.130 and 511.140, the plaintiff may, if he demand it, have a jury to assess his damages. If no jury be demanded the court shall assess the damages, or give the other relief asked in the petition, and final judgment shall be given thereon.

See also : 511.130 (mo-rsmo), 511.140 (mo-rsmo)

Sumpter v. J. E. Sieben Construction Co., 492 S.W.2d 150 (Mo. App. 1973)

Missouri Court of Appeals · 1973-03-05 · No. 25943 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing § 511.110 RSMo, § 511.150 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Section 511.110, V.A.M.S., entitles a plaintiff to an interlocutory judgment of default when the defendant has failed to file a timely answer or other pleading.
This procedure has been preserved by Section 511.150, V.A.M.S., which, by its terms allows a plaintiff, if he demands it, a jury to assess damages against a defaulting defendant when the damages are not ascertainable by a written instrument.
