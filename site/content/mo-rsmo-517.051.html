517.051 — When case to be tried.

in forceIn force since : 1987-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=517.051 · Interactive browser

Current text

Every case shall be tried upon the return date of the summons, when the summons has been duly and timely served, or on a date to which the case has been continued.

Neal v. Director of Revenue, 312 S.W.3d 444 (Mo. App. S.D. 2010)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Southern District · 2010-05-26 · No. SD 29758 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Southern District opinion applying or construing § 517.071 RSMo, § 517.011 RSMo, § 517.051 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Section 517.051 provides that “[e]very case shall be tried upon the return date of the summons, when the summons has been duly and timely served, or on a date to which the case has been continued.” Section 517.071.1 provides that “[a] ease shall be continued to a day certain upon the request of any party made on or before the return date of the summons.”
Section 517.011 states that the provisions of chapter 517 apply to all cases arising under chapter 577.
We find the legislature’s use in section 517.071 of both “shall” and “may” in connection with requests for continuances made under different circumstances clearly indicates an intent to make mandatory the continuance of a matter when the request meets the requirements of section 517.071.1.

State ex rel. Johnston v. Luckenbill, 975 S.W.2d 253 (Mo. App. W.D. 1998)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 1998-09-01 · No. WD 54398 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing Rule 74.06, § 517.051 RSMo, § 517.071 RSMo, § 517.011 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Johnston contends that the circuit court should have entered an order mandating that the associate circuit division grant his Rule 74.06 motion and to set aside its judgment.
Section 517.051 says, “Every ease shall be tried upon the return date of the summons, when the summons has been duly and timely served, or on a date to which the case has been continued.” Section 517.071 authorizes the associate circuit division to continue a case.
Because the associate circuit division acted without authority, we issue a writ of mandamus mandating that the associate circuit division set aside its judgment against Johnston in Case Na CV1096-163AC. BRECKENRIDGE, P.J., and LOWENSTEIN, J., concur. . Section 517.011.1(3) makes the procedural requirements of Chapter 517 applicable to "[a]ll cases arising under chapter ... 535, RSMo[.]” All citations to statutes refer to the 1994 Revised Statutes. . The General Assembly had not enacted §§ 535.030 and 535.040, RSMo Supp.1997, when the deputy served Johnston. . Judge Luckenbill relies on Plaza Point Investments, Inc. v. Dunnaway, 637 S.W.2d 303 (Mo. App.1982), to contend that a continuance may include moving a hearing to a date earlier than the original hearing date.
