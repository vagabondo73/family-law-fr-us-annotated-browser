513.360 — Proceedings to set aside or quash execution.

in forceIn force since : 1939-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=513.360 · Interactive browser

Current text

If any person against whose property any execution or order of sale shall be issued, apply to any judge of the court out of which the same may have been issued, by petition, verified by oath or affirmation, setting forth good cause why same ought to be stayed, set aside or quashed, reasonable notice of such intended application being previously given to the opposite party, his attorney of record or agent, such judge shall thereupon hear the complaint.

State ex rel. Auto Finance Co. v. Collins, 496 S.W.2d 827 (Mo. 1973)

Supreme Court of Missouri · 1973-07-16 · No. 58021 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Supreme Court of Missouri opinion applying or construing § 513.360 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Section 513.360 provides: “If any person against whose property any execution or order of sale shall be issued, apply to any judge of the court out of which the same may have been issued, by petition, verified by oath or affirmation, setting forth good cause why same ought to be stayed, set aside or quashed, reasonable notice of such intended application being previously given to the opposite party, his attorney of record or agent, such judge shall thereupon hear the complaint.”
Sec. 513.360 has been in force in Missouri since at least 1855 (Ch. 63, Sec. 69, RSMo 1855); and in 1902 and 1904, when Brownfield and Carr were decided it was Ch. 26, Sec. 3223, RSMo 1899, which applied only to courts of record.
Sec. 513.360, supra, has been part of the Missouri law relating to executions in courts of record since 1885, but until 1945, there was no similar provision in the laws governing executions in justice courts, nor was there any law making the procedure on execution in courts of record applicable to justice courts.
