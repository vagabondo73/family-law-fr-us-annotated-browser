506.372 — Monthly payments.

in forceIn force since : 1999-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=506.372 · Interactive browser

Current text

If the court has entered an order for payment of fees in installments pursuant to section 506.369, the offender shall make monthly payments to the department of corrections of twenty percent of the preceding month's income credited to the offender's account until the fees are paid in full. The department of corrections shall forward payments from the offender's account to the department of revenue for deposit to the general revenue fund until the fees are paid in full. The department of corrections shall establish written guidelines for the priority of payment consistent with state and federal law.

See also : 506.369 (mo-rsmo)

Davis v. Kempker, 167 S.W.3d 721 (Mo. App. W.D. 2005)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 2005-06-21 · No. WD 64237 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing § 506.366 RSMo, § 506.369 RSMo, § 506.372 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Section 506.366 provides that “[a]n offender seeking to bring a civil action or to appeal a judgment in a civil action without the prepayment of fees ... shall submit a request to the court to proceed without the prepayment of fees.”
Finally, Section 506.372 provides that “[i]f the court has entered an order for payment of fees in installments pursuant to section 506.369, the offender shall make monthly payments to the department of corrections of twenty percent of the preceding month’s income credited to the offender’s account until the fees are paid in full.”
Section 506.366 provides: An offender seeking to bring a civil action or to appeal a judgment in a civil action without the prepayment of fees or security due to indigency shall submit a request to the court to proceed without the prepayment of fees.

Downs v. Director of Adult Institutions, 40 S.W.3d 19 (Mo. App. W.D. 2001)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 2001-03-06 · No. WD 58753 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing § 506.372 RSMo, Rule 84.04 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

William Downs appeals from the circuit court’s order dismissing, for failure to state a claim upon which relief could be granted, his petition for declaratory judgment in which he sought a declaration by the court that the respondent, George Lombardi, the Director of Adult Institutions of the Missouri Department of Corrections, had acted unlawfully in allowing his inmate account to be debited for payment of filing fees for civil lawsuits filed by the appellant, as authorized by § 506.372 and § 217.260.6, such that his account was reduced to a zero balance.
Compliance with Rule 84.04 briefing requirements is mandatory in order to ensure that appellate courts do not become advocates by speculating on facts and on arguments that have not been made.
Rule 84.04(d) “ ‘sets forth a form for a point relied on that satisfies the [rule’s] requirements.’ ” Id. (quoting In re Marriage of Wright, 990 S.W.2d 703, 708 (Mo.App.1999)).
