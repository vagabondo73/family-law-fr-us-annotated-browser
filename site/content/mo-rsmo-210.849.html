210.849 — Birth records.

in forceIn force since : 1987-07-15 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=210.849 · Interactive browser

Current text

Upon order of a court of this state or upon request of a court or authorized administrative agency of another state, the bureau shall prepare an amended birth certificate consistent with the findings of the court.

Jenkins v. Austin, 255 S.W.3d 24 (Mo. App. W.D. 2008)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 2008-05-20 · No. WD 68302 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing § 210.841, § 210.849, 95.01 RSMo (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

This Court held that the provision of § 210.841 empowering the circuit court to enter judgment on “any matter in the best interest of the child” granted authority to the circuit court to order a name change.
Further noting that § 210.849 directed that birth certificates be amended consistent with the findings of the court, we held that “[e]learly, a change of name was contemplated pursuant to the provisions of the [Uniform Parentage] Act.”
Outside of the dissolution context, a child’s name may be changed by the child’s petitioning, by and through a next friend, pursuant to section 527.270, RSMo 1994, and Rule 95.01; see also Rule 95.02-03.
No Missouri court has previously been called upon to decide whether the court in a dissolution proceeding has authority to change the name of a child of the marriage, or whether such a name change can be accomplished only through a change of name petition filed under section 527.270 and Rules 95.01 et seq.
