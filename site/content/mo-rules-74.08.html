74.08 — Judgment Lien

in forceIn force since : 2003-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199748 · Rule 74 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

Except as provided in Chapter 454, RSMo, or Chapter 517, RSMo, the lien of a judgment commences upon entry of the judgment, continues for a period of ten years, and is revived by a revival of the judgment.

Issues : mo.procedure.judgments, proc.mo.judgments.liens-satisfaction
