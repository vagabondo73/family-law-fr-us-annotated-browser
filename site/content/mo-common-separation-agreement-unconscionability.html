separation-agreement-unconscionability — Separation agreements — binding effect and unconscionability review (§ 452.325)

in forceIn force since : 2025-03-11 · Applicable : MO

Summary rule

“Generally speaking, the terms of a separation agreement in a proceeding for dissolution of marriage are binding upon the court unless the court finds the separation agreement is unconscionable.” (Tierney v. Tierney, 2025). “An agreement is unconscionable if it involves “an inequality so strong, gross, and manifest that it must be impossible to state it to one with common sense without producing an exclamation at the inequality of it.”” (Landers v. Sgouros, 2007; In re Marriage of Thompson, 2000). The test “is not measured by the factors delineated in § 452.330” (Thompson, quoting In re Marriage of Turner).
