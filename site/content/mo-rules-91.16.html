91.16 — Production of Restrained Person at Hearing

in forceIn force since : 1983-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199908 · Rule 91 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

The person upon whom the writ is served shall produce the restrained person at the hearing unless the court otherwise orders.

#### Committee Note – 1983

The source is prior Rule 91.18.

Compare: 28 U.S.C. Section 2243.

See also : 91.18 (mo-rules)
