99.14 — Court to Allow Charges for Taking and Delivering Property

in forceIn force since : 1977-12-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=200000 · Rule 99 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

The court shall tax as costs the reasonable expenses and charges incurred by the sheriff or other officer incident to the taking and delivery of the property.

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is substantially the same as prior Rule 99.20.

Issues : proc.mo.provisional-remedies.replevin

Binding interpretations (1)

Riddle v. Dean Machinery Co., 564 S.W.2d 238 (Mo. App. 1978)

Missouri Court of Appeals · 1978-01-30 · KCD 28356, KCD 28358, KCD 28484 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing Rule 99.14 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

This portion of the judgment comports with Rule 99.14 which contemplates that upon failure of the defense, the verdict and judgment in a suit for recovery of possession of personal property (replevin) (a) will find and declare the prevailing party’s right of possession, (b) determine and assess value of the property at time of trial, thus to afford the prevailing party an election to take the property or its assessed value, and (c) determine and assess damages, if any, for taking and detention.
