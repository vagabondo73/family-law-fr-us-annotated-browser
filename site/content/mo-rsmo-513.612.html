513.612 — Right to file motion to dismiss — jury trial.

in forceIn force since : 1986-03-17 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=513.612 · Interactive browser

Current text

Any party may bring one motion to dismiss at any time and such motion shall be heard and ruled on within ten days. Any party may demand a jury trial.

State v. Scott, 933 S.W.2d 884 (Mo. App. W.D. 1996)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 1996-10-15 · No. WD 51932 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing § 513.612 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

We, therefore, order that the guilty plea and sentencing transcripts be stricken from the record on appeal. In his first point, Scott claims that the trial court lacked jurisdiction to adjudicate the state’s forfeiture action because it failed to convene a hearing on his motion to dismiss within the 10-day time limit set forth in § 513.612.
The trial court did not hear or rule on the motion until November 1, 1995, when it ordered Scott’s property to be forfeited. Scott argues that the trial court lost jurisdiction over the forfeiture action because the court did not rule on the motion within 10 days as mandated by § 513.612.

State v. Eberenz, 805 S.W.2d 359 (Mo. App. 1991)

Missouri Court of Appeals · 1991-03-19 · No. 58668 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing § 513.607 RSMo, § 513.612 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

The provisions of § 513.607.5(2) require a seizing authority to notify a prosecuting authority within three days of seizure “and the prosecuting attorney or attorney general shall, within five days after receiving notice of seizure, file a petition for forfeiture.”
The motion is expressly authorized by the provisions of § 513.612.
The issue in this case is whether the provisions of § 513.607.5(2) are mandatory or directory.

State v. Hampton, 817 S.W.2d 470 (Mo. App. W.D. 1991)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 1991-02-19 · No. WD 43204 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing § 513.612 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Section 513.612, which allows “[a]ny party to bring a motion to dismiss at anytime and such motion shall be ruled on within ten days,” need not be considered. This section merely reiterates an intent by the legislature to effectuate a speedy disposition to litigation involving contraband.
