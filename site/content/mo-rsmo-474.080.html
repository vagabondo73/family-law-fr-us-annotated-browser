474.080 — Issue of void or dissolved marriage, legitimate.

in forceIn force since : 1955-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=474.080 · Interactive browser

Current text

The issue of all marriages deemed null in law, or dissolved by divorce, are legitimate.

Issues : mo.succession.intestacy

Binding interpretations (1)

Traders Bank of Kansas City v. Goulding, 711 S.W.2d 872 (Mo. banc 1986)

Supreme Court of Missouri · 1986-06-17 · No. 67699 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Supreme Court of Missouri opinion applying or construing § 474.060, § 474.070, § 474.080 RSMo (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

Section 474.060 provides in subsection 2, inter alia, that a person born out of wedlock is a child of the father if paternity is established by clear and convincing proof.
Meffert, 287 Mo. 366, 229 S.W. 762 (1921), which preceded it, involve the construction of §§ 474.070 and 474.080, RSMo 1978, then §§ 315 and 316, RSMo 1939.
Section 474.080 provides that the issue of a marriage deemed null in law are legitimate.
Also mentioned is the fact that § 474.060, which did not originally provide for intestate succession of illegitimate children to interests in their father’s estate, was later amended conforming to pronouncements of the United States Supreme Court.
