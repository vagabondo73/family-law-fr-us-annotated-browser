maintenance-two-step — Maintenance: two-step threshold (need) and balancing (ability to pay)

in forceIn force since : 2025-06-27 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/file.jsp?id=221633 · Leading source: Archdekin v. Archdekin, 562 S.W.3d 298 (Mo. banc 2018) · Leading source: CLAIRE S. WILKERSON v. CLAY M. WILKERSON, No. SD38454 (Mo. App. S.D. 2025) · Leading source: Joy Janelle McVean, Respondent, vs. Anthony David McVean, Appellant., No. ED112557 (Mo. App. E.D. 2025) · Interactive browser

Summary rule

Section 452.335 “provides a two-step process for an award of maintenance.” (Archdekin v. Archdekin, Mo. banc 2018). “As a prerequisite to awarding spousal maintenance, section 452.335 requires the party seeking maintenance to establish he or she "[l]acks sufficient property, including marital property apportioned to him , to provide for his reasonable needs."” (Archdekin). “When a party requests maintenance, the trial court must follow a two-step process to determine if maintenance is warranted.” (Wilkerson, 2025). “If the trial court determines the party seeking maintenance cannot meet their reasonable needs, the trial court must apply the factors of Section 452.335.2” so as to balance the reasonable needs of the spouse seeking maintenance against the ability of the other spouse to pay (McVean, 2025).
