relocation-burden — Relocation of a child: burden on the relocating parent (good faith and best interests)

in forceIn force since : 2003-12-09 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courtlistener.com/c/S.W.3d/121/562/ · Leading source: Herigon v. Herigon, 121 S.W.3d 562 (Mo. App. W.D. 2003) · Leading source: Dorman v. Dorman, 91 S.W.3d 167 (Mo. App. W.D. 2002) · Interactive browser

Summary rule

Section 452.377.9 “drops the requirement that the non-relocating parent show good faith in opposing the move and simply requires the relocating parent to shoulder the burden of establishing good faith in proposing the move.” (Herigon v. Herigon, 2003). Relocation is allowed where the court finds that the “proposed relocation was made in good faith and is in the best interest of the minor child” (Dorman v. Dorman, 2002).

Rule sources : Herigon v. Herigon, 121 S.W.3d 562 (Mo. App. W.D. 2003), Dorman v. Dorman, 91 S.W.3d 167 (Mo. App. W.D. 2002)
