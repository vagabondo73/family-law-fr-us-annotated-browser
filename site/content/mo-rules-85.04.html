85.04 — Issuance of Writ of Attachment

in forceIn force since : 1981-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199811 · Rule 85 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

If the court finds that the facts stated in the affidavit show that the writ of attachment should issue, the writ shall be issued upon compliance with Rule 85.08.

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is new.

Compare: Prior Rule 85.04.

See also : 85.08 (mo-rules)

Union State Bank of Clinton v. Dolan, 718 S.W.2d 522 (Mo. App. W.D. 1986)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 1986-08-12 · No. WD 37548 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing Rule 99.04, Rule 99.06, Rule 99.11, Rule 85.07, Rule 85.04, Rule 85.11 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

There is no requirement that the court make specific finding of facts stated in the affidavit under Rule 99.04.
Under Rule 99.06, the purpose of the delivery bond is to prosecute the action without delay, for the return of the property to defendant, or in default thereof, to pay all damages for injuries to the property and for its taking and detention and for all costs.
If this were a jurisdictional matter, there would be no reason for Rule 99.11, the purpose of which is to protect a defendant with a sufficient replevin bond by a curative remedy.

Salenia A.B. v. Air National Aircraft Sales & Services, Inc., 712 S.W.2d 386 (Mo. App. W.D. 1986)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 1986-04-08 · No. WD 36940 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing Rule 85.04, Rule 41.02, Rule 85.07 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

In fact, Salenia did not file an approved surety bond in the amount of $1,000,000 until August 8,1984, the day the aircraft was flown to Luxembourg and 13 days after the writ of attachment issued. Salenia’s failure to file a bond until August 8, 1984, ignores the plain language of Rule 85.04 and 85.-08(a), which mandates the furnishing of a bond before the writ of attachment issues.
Rule 41.02 states: “Rules 41 to 101, inclusive, are promulgated pursuant to authority granted this Court by Section 5 of article V of the constitution of Missouri and supersede all statutes and existing court rules inconsistent therewith.”
A review of Missouri cases indicates the authority for this requirement is found in § 521.170(1), RSMo 1978, and corresponding Rule 85.07.
