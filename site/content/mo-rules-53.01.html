53.01 — Commencement of Civil Action

in forceIn force since : 1972-12-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199607 · Interactive browser

Current text

A civil action is commenced by filing a petition with the court.
