451.030 — Bigamous marriage void.

in forceIn force since : 1939-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=451.030 · Interactive browser

Current text

All marriages, where either of the parties has a former wife or husband living, shall be void, unless the former marriage shall have been dissolved.
