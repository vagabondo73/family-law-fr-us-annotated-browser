452.875 — Service of petition and order.

in forceIn force since : 2009-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=452.875 · Interactive browser

Current text

Except as otherwise provided in section 452.885, the petition and order shall be served by any method authorized by the laws of this state upon the respondent and any person who has physical custody of the child.

See also : 452.885 (mo-rsmo)
