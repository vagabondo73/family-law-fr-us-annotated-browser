512.030 — Appeals jointly or separately.

in forceIn force since : 1943-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=512.030 · Interactive browser

Current text

Parties interested jointly, severally, or otherwise in a judgment may join in an appeal therefrom; or, without summons and severance any one or more of them may appeal separately or any two or more of them may join in an appeal.

Correspondences (CPC ↔ Missouri)

art. 552 · Légifrance512.030partial—Article 552 governs the effects of an appeal brought by or against one of several jointly liable or indivisible parties, including joinder of co-interested parties. RSMo 512.030 allows jointly or severally interested parties to appeal jointly or separately, but does not provide the same reservation effects or court-ordered joinder.
art. 553 · Légifrance512.030partial—Article 553 provides that an appeal by one party affects the others in an indivisible case and that an appeal against one is admissible only if all are joined. RSMo 512.030 permits joint or separate appeals by interested parties, but contains no equivalent indivisibility rule or all-party requirement.
art. 609 · Légifrance512.020, 512.030partial—Article 609 allows any interested party to appeal even when the unfavorable ruling does not benefit the opposing party. Missouri permits an appeal by an aggrieved party and separate appeals by interested parties, but uses the materially different aggrievement criterion.
art. 615 · Légifrance81.02, 512.030partial—Article 615 governs the effect of an appeal in indivisible litigation and requires all affected parties to be called. Rule 81.02 and RSMo 512.030 allow parties to join an appeal or appeal separately, but do not provide the same effects or mandatory collective participation.
art. 900 · Légifrance81.02, 81.04, 512.030, 512.050partial—An appeal may be brought by unilateral declaration or by joint application. Missouri permits separate or joint appeals and requires a notice of appeal, but does not reproduce the French two-form mechanism exactly.
art. 926 · Légifrance81.02, 512.030partial—Article 926 permits a joint appeal only when all parties to the trial proceedings present it. Missouri law allows parties to join an appeal, but does not require every party to the judgment to join.
art. 1239-3 · Légifrance512.020, 512.030partial—The article allows every family-council member and the guardianship judge to appeal, regardless of their vote. RSMo 512.020 permits an aggrieved party to appeal, and RSMo 512.030 allows joint or separate appeals, but neither reproduces these specific appellant categories.
