child-support-reciprocity — France–United States reciprocity for the enforcement of child support (42 U.S.C. § 659a foreign reciprocating country)

Not listed as a treaty in Treaties in Force 2026. Since 1 January 2017 the 2007 Hague Child Support Convention (int-hcch-2007) applies between the United States and the European Union Member States including France; see int-hcch-2007.
