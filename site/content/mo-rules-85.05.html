85.05 — Form of Writ of Attachment

in forceIn force since : 2013-07-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199812 · Rule 85 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

A writ of attachment shall be in the following form:

The State of Missouri to any sheriff of any county in the State of Missouri.

Whereas, AB has on the ________ day of ________, 20___, obtained a writ of attachment against CD, you are commanded to execute this writ by attaching the property of CD or as much thereof as will be sufficient to satisfy the sum $________, with interest and costs and on the ________ day of ________, 20___, certify to this court how you executed this writ. _________________

EF, Clerk

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is new.

Compare: Prior Rule 85.17.

See also : 85.17 (mo-rules)
