517.161 — Procedure in certain cases filed prior to January 1, 1987 — effective date of certain sections.

in forceIn force since : 1985-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=517.161 · Interactive browser

Current text

The provisions of sections 66.060, 478.225, 482.325, 482.365, 506.010, 512.180, 517.011 to 517.151, 521.420, 535.020, 535.040, 535.110, 535.160 and 546.595 shall become effective January 1, 1987. Any case filed prior to January 1, 1987, in the divisions of the circuit court presided over by an associate circuit judge shall be governed by the practice and procedure of causes heard by associate circuit judges as were in effect on the date the case was filed, including the right to trial de novo.

See also : 506.010 (mo-rsmo), 512.180 (mo-rsmo)

Larimer v. Robertson, 800 S.W.2d 154 (Mo. App. 1990)

Missouri Court of Appeals · 1990-12-11 · No. 16803 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing § 517.111 RSMo, § 517.161 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

This was contrary to § 517.111.2, RSMo 1986, which reads: When a ease is tried before a judge without a jury, judgment shall be entered by the judge within thirty days after the case is submitted for final decision unless the parties consent to a longer period of time.
Section 517.111.2 applies to the instant case by reason of §§ 517.011.1 and 517.161, RSMo 1986.
Plaintiffs, in their brief, suggest that when defendant requested this court to retain jurisdiction and enter judgment under Rule 84.14, such request amounted to a consent by defendant to waive the time limitations imposed by § 517.111.2.
