91.20 — Order of Remand or Other Disposition

in forceIn force since : 1983-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199912 · Rule 91 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

If legal cause is shown for the restraint, the person shall be returned to the restraint from which he was taken or committed to such other restraint as may be proper.

#### Committee Note – 1983

The sources are prior Rules 91.32 and 91.43.

State v. White, 838 S.W.2d 140 (Mo. App. W.D. 1992)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 1992-08-25 · No. WD 45326 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing Rule 91.18, Rule 91.20 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Further, “[i]f no legal cause is shown for the restraint, the court shall forthwith order the person discharged,” Rule 91.18, and “[i]f legal cause is shown ... the person shall be returned to the restraint from which he was taken or committed to such other restraint as may be proper.”
Admittedly, the proper procedure in a post-conviction habeas case would appear to be Rule 91.20, and a “return” of the petitioner to the court of the underlying conviction, but the discharge that occurred in this case does not deny the Clay County court is power to set aside the unlawful judgment, and proceed upon the lawful charges.
