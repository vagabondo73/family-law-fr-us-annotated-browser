96.08 — Interests of Parties to be Determined

in forceIn force since : 1981-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199946 · Rule 96 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

The court shall determine the interests of the parties and order partition in kind or the sale of the land.

#### Committee Note – 1981

This is substantially the same as prior Rule 96.16.

As an interlocutory order of sale under section 512.020(4), the partition order needed to determine the “rights of the parties,” meaning the quantum of interest held by Bramlett and Reagan.
