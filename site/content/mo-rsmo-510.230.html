510.230 — General verdict, when rendered.

in forceIn force since : 1943-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=510.230 · Interactive browser

Current text

In every issue for the recovery of money only, or specific real or personal property, the jury shall render a general verdict.
