455.070 — Proceedings independent of others.

in forceIn force since : 1980-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=455.070 · Interactive browser

Current text

All proceedings under sections 455.010 to 455.085 are independent of any proceedings for dissolution of marriage, legal separation, separate maintenance and other actions between the parties and are in addition to any other available civil or criminal remedies, unless otherwise specifically provided herein.

N.M. v. Martin, 508 S.W.3d 171 (Mo. App. W.D. 2016)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 2016-11-29 · WD 79313 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing § 455.070 RSMo (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

Pursuant to Section 455.070, RSMo 2000, “All proceedings under sections 455.010 to 455.085 are independent of any proceedings for dissolution of marriage, legal separation, separate maintenance and other actions between the parties and are in addition to any other available civil or criminal remedies, unless otherwise specifically provided herein.”
In this case, Martin’s counterclaim for damages was an “other action” between the parties that could not be part of the adult abuse case under Section 455.070.
