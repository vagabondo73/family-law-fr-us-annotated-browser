Summary rule

“Section 452.355 authorizes the circuit court to award attorney fees while taking into consideration “all relevant factors including the financial resources of both parties, the merits of the case and the actions of the parties during the pendency of the action....” (Goins v. Goins v. Goins, 406 S.W.3d 886 (Mo. banc 2013)

Supreme Court of Missouri · 2013-07-16 · No. SC 92672 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Supreme Court of Missouri opinion applying or construing § 452.355, § 452.300, § 452.415 RSMo (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

Section 452.355 authorizes the circuit court to award attorney fees while taking into consideration “all relevant factors including the financial resources of both parties, the merits of the case and the actions of the parties during the pendency of the action.... ” The statute further authorizes the circuit court to award fees for “legal services rendered and costs incurred prior to the commencement of the proceeding and after entry of a final judgment.”
In relevant part, section 452.355.1 provides: [u]nless otherwise indicated, the court from time to time after considering all relevant factors including the financial resources of both parties, the merits of the case and the actions of the parties during the pendency of the action, may order a party to pay a reasonable amount for the cost to the other party of maintaining or defending any proceeding pursuant to sections 452.300 to 452.415 and for attorney’s fees, including sums for legal services rendered and costs incurred prior to the commencement of the proceeding and after entry of a final judgment.
Goins’ first point on appeal asserts that section 452.355 is unconstitutional to the extent that the statute grants the circuit court subject matter jurisdiction to enter an award of attorney fees on appeal while the appeal is pending.
“Section 452.355.1 permits the trial court to award attorneys’ fees on appeal of a dissolution decree.”

Kohring v. Snodgrass, 999 S.W.2d 228 (Mo. banc 1999)

Supreme Court of Missouri · 1999-08-24 · No. 81139 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Supreme Court of Missouri opinion applying or construing § 452.355 RSMo (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

Under section 452.355.1, RSMo Supp.1998, the court is authorized to award attorney fees “after considering all relevant factors including the financial resources of both parties .... ” On appeal, father bears the burden of proving that the award constituted an abuse of discretion.
