55.04 — Technical Forms Not Required in Pleading

in forceIn force since : 1973-09-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=199635 · Rule 55 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

Each averment of a pleading shall be simple, concise and direct. No technical forms of pleading or motions are required.

Hester v. Barnett, 723 S.W.2d 544 (Mo. App. W.D. 1987)

Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District · 1987-01-20 · No. WD 36517 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Western District opinion applying or construing Rule 55.12, Rule 55.04 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Rule 55.12 allows a pleader to adopt by reference statements “in a different part of the same pleading or in another pleading or in any motion.”
The adoption by reference, however, must subserve the dominant objective of the rules of pleading: that “[e]ach averment of a pleading shall be simple, concise and direct” [Rule 55.04], and that “[a] pleading ... shall contain ... a short and plain statement of the facts ...” [Rule 55.05].
The attempted incorporation by reference by allusion to “those actions described specifically in other Counts” does not inform the defendant as to the nature and extent of the incorporation, and hence is ineffective as an adoption by reference under Rule 55.12.
