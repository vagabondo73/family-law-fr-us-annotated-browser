516.371 — Limitation on action for sexual contact by certain persons.

in forceIn force since : 1989-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=516.371 · Interactive browser

Current text

Notwithstanding any provision of law to the contrary, there shall be a ten-year statute of limitation on any action for damages for personal injury caused to an individual by a person within the third degree of affinity or consanguinity who subjects such individual to sexual contact, as defined in section 566.010.
