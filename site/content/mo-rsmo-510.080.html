510.080 — Court may continue an action to a fixed day.

in forceIn force since : 1943-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=510.080 · Interactive browser

Current text

For good cause shown, the court may continue an action to a fixed day, or to a date for trial to be set thereafter. Every continuance granted on the application of a party may be at the cost of such party, if so ordered by the court.
