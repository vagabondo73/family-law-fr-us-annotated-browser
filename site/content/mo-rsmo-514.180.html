514.180 — Court may make order for payment of unpaid costs, when.

in forceIn force since : 1943-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=514.180 · Interactive browser

Current text

If a plaintiff who has once dismissed an action in any court commences an action based upon or including the same claim against the same defendant, the court may make an order for the payment of any unpaid costs of the action previously dismissed and may stay the proceedings in the action until the plaintiff has complied with the order.

Gasper v. St. Charles County, 857 S.W.2d 370 (Mo. App. 1993)

Missouri Court of Appeals · 1993-05-18 · No. 62701 published
Basis (a): decided on or after the current version

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing § 516.230 RSMo, Rule 53.01, § 514.180 RSMo (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Specifically, the court found the filing of plaintiffs’ petition in the second action did not constitute a “commencement” within the meaning of § 516.230 RSMo 1986, since the cause could not be commenced until plaintiff paid the court costs from the first suit.
Pursuant to § 516.230 RSMo 1986, plaintiffs were entitled to commence a new action within one year of the dismissal. A civil action is commenced when the petition is filed. Rule 53.01; Peltzman v. Beachner, 813 S.W.2d 932, 933 (Mo.App.1991).
Section 514.180 RSMo 1986 provides the court with the same authority.
