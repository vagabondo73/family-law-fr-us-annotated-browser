81.13 — Agreed Statement as the Record on Appeal

in forceIn force since : 1984-01-01 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=200316 · Rule 81 — all subdivisions (courts.mo.gov) · Interactive browser

Current text

When the questions presented by an appeal can be determined without an examination of all the pleadings, evidence and proceedings in the court below, the parties may prepare and sign a statement of the case showing how the questions arose and were decided in the trial court and setting forth only so many of the facts averred and proved or sought to be proved as are essential to a decision of the questions by the appellate court. The statement shall include a copy of the judgment or order appealed from, a copy of the notice of appeal with its filing date and a concise statement of the points to be relied on by the appellant. If the statement conforms to the truth, it, together with such additions as the Court may consider necessary fully to present the questions raised by the appeal, shall be approved by the trial court and shall then be certified to the appellate court as the record on appeal.

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals opinion applying or construing Rule 81.13, Rule 81.12 (neutral index summary; see verbatim excerpts).

Rule 81.13 provides a means for parties to set forth an agreed statement as to the record on appeal: When the questions presented by an appeal can be determined without an examination of all the pleadings, evidence and proceedings in the court below, the parties may prepare and sign a statement of the case showing how the questions arose and were decided in the trial court and setting forth only so many of the facts averred and proved or sought to be proved as are essential to a decision of the questions by the appellate court.
If a party petitions this Court for review of an issue, then it is his duty to provide “all of the record, proceedings and evidence necessary to the determination of all questions to be presented.” Rule 81.12(a); see also Davis v. Long, 521 S.W.2d 7, 8-9 (Mo.App.Spfld.D.1975).
If the statement conforms to the truth, it, together with such additions as the Court may consider necessary fully to present the questions raised by the appeal, shall be approved by the trial court and shall then be certified to the appellate court as the record on appeal. Rule 81.13.
