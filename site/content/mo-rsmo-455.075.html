455.075 — Attorney's fees, cost.

in forceIn force since : 2022-08-28 · Applicable : MO

Official source : https://revisor.mo.gov/main/OneSection.aspx?section=455.075 · Revisor — this version (bid) · Interactive browser

Current text

The court may order a party to pay a reasonable amount to the other party for attorney's fees incurred prior to the commencement of the proceeding, throughout the proceeding, and after entry of judgment. The court shall consider all relevant factors, including the financial resources of both parties, and may order that the amount be paid directly to the attorney, who may enforce the order in his name.

Correspondences (CPC ↔ Missouri)

CPCMissouriEquivalenceFRCPNote
art. 699 · Légifrance452.355, 454.1584, 455.075partial—The CPC allows counsel, where representation is mandatory, to recover directly from the losing party costs advanced without a retainer, subject to legal setoff. The Missouri provisions permit direct payment and enforcement by counsel in specified proceedings, but do not establish the same general advance-cost and setoff mechanism.
art. 700 · Légifrance452.890, 452.355, 455.075partial—The CPC permits awards for expenses outside taxable costs, including attorney fees, based on equity and the losing party’s economic circumstances, and allows the court to deny an award. Missouri provides costs and fees for prevailing parties or based on financial resources and circumstances, but in specialized proceedings and without the same legal-aid or minimum-award rules.

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District opinion applying or construing § 455.075, § 452.355 RSMo (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

Pursuant to Section 455.075, the trial court may order a party to pay a reasonable amount to the other party for attorney’s fees in adult abuse cases.
The language of this statute essentially mirrors the language of Section 452.355 of the Dissolution of Marriage Act.
Under the analogous section 452.355, “a court must know what debts each party owes and what non-employment income each party has before it can determine either need or ability to pay.”

Summary (non-official) : Missouri Court of Appeals, Eastern District opinion applying or construing § 455.075 RSMo (neutral index summary; see excerpts).

In determining the issue of attorney’s fees in an adult abuse case the court is directed by § 455.075 to “consider all relevant factors, including the financial resources of both parties....” Wife argues that the trial court’s statement concerning the circumstances which caused the husband to initiate the modification proceedings indicates the sole basis for the court’s ruling and, therefore, the court failed to follow the statutory mandate to consider the financial resources of both parties.
She claims that the Court did not consider all relevant factors, including the financial resources of the parties, pursuant to § 455.075, RSMo.1986, in making its ruling.
